# edge_left · 贴边（左）

用途: 拖到屏幕左边缘时，缩到屏幕外只露半身扒着边。图片左边框 = 屏幕左边缘。
规则: 通用, 贴边
方向: left
成对: edge_right
导入: SKIP_EDGE=left
默认动作: Hiding beyond the LEFT border and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. A gentle curious look.
