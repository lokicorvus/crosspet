# friend_up · 关系升级

用途: 多桌宠里一对角色刚升到新的关系（认识了 / 好朋友 / 挚友）时，两只一起换上这张、各说一句升级台词，程序在她身边飘心。没有这张时用 happy。
规则: 通用
默认动作: Touched and happy about a new friendship: cheeks blushing, eyes softly curved in a warm, slightly shy smile; holding a small soft heart-shaped plush cushion (pastel pink, a real object in her hands, not a floating symbol) against her chest with both hands, shoulders lifted a little as if her heart just skipped.
