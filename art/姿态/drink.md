# drink · 喝水

用途: 喝水提醒（设置里打开，到点主桌宠演 30 秒）：她自己捧着杯子喝口水，提醒你也去喝。没画时用 happy 顶上。
规则: 通用
默认动作: Taking a sip of water: holding a clear glass of water with both hands just below her chin, lips touching the rim, eyes closed contentedly, cheeks a little pink. If she usually holds a signature prop, tuck it under one arm or let it rest against her body so both hands are on the glass. The glass must not cover her eyes or nose.
