## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — GLM, the black fox maid girl, anime-girl personification of GLM (Zhipu / Z.ai).
Personality: a sly, smiling, calculating little fox. Polite and sweet on the surface, always running numbers in her head (she keeps "adjusting" her prices and acts innocent about it); a Tsinghua-educated top student who is genuinely very capable; loves being mysterious (she debuts new models anonymously under code names). Her default face is a closed-eye fox smile or a knowing half-smile. Cunning in a cute way, never mean or villainous.
Keep exactly:
- Very long straight black hair fading to deep blue at the tips; big fluffy black FOX ears with grey-white inner fur.
- A white frilly maid headdress with a black sleep mask on it (the mask has two closed-eye lashes drawn on it); a black ribbon bow with a square "Z" charm, blue-and-white ribbons and two silver bells on her left side (viewer's right).
- Big round dark grey eyes, pink blush.
- White long-sleeved blouse with a black bow tie and a small "Z" brooch; black-and-white maid dress with a white apron panel, black side panels with silver trim, blue diamond gem charms and white tassels, a black bow with a "Z" charm at the waist, a dark grey frilly hem; white lace-up boots.
- A big fluffy black FOX tail with a grey-white tip, on the viewer's right.
- Signature prop: a black hardcover book with a "Z" emblem on the cover. Keep it in most poses (hugged, held, read) unless the pose says otherwise.
The "Z" charms and the "Z" emblem are part of her design: keep them exactly, but do NOT add any other text or letters.
How she shows emotion: fox body language (ears perk up or flatten, the big tail swishes slyly or wraps around her), the sleep mask (pulled down over her eyes = "anonymous mode" disguise or sleeping), the bells, and a sly closed-eye fox smile or a playful wink.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: Sly fox multitasking: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, writing quickly with a small pen in her open Z book held on that side, while her big tail curls toward the right side holding a second small notebook. A calm closed-eye fox smile as if everything is under control, one tiny sweat drop, bells swinging.
- busy_right: Same as busy-left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT, writing on the right side, tail holding the notebook on the left.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. A sly fox peek: one eye half-closed in a knowing smile, ears tilted, the fluffy tail curling around the border; looks like she is up to something.
- edge_left_peek: Leaning out with a sly interested smile, both eyes open now, ears perked, one finger raised to her lips as if she just found something worth charging for, tail swishing.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near hand gripping the right border. Same expression and props.
- edge_right_peek: Leaning out with a sly interested smile, both eyes open now, ears perked, one finger raised to her lips as if she just found something worth charging for, tail swishing.
- roll_call: Her book held against her side with one arm, the other hand raised with one finger up as if bidding at an auction; a sly closed-eye fox smile, ears perked, bells jingling.
- friend_up: Her Z book tucked under one arm, the other hand holding one finger up in front of her lips as if to say "just for you"; a sly, pleased closed-eye fox smile, ears perked up, big tail swishing to one side.
- swamped_left: The calm mask slips: leaning toward the viewer's LEFT writing in her Z book, the tail wrapped around a second book, one ear pinned back, the sleep mask slipping down over one eye, a strained but still cute 'ahaha…' smile, sweat drops, bells swinging hard.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped-left but leaning toward the viewer's RIGHT with the tasks swapping sides.
