# DeepSeek

角色 id: deepseek
参考图: characters/deepseek/idle.webp, docs/prompts/ref-deepseek.webp, characters/deepseek/portrait.webp

## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek, the blue whale maid girl, anime-girl personification of DeepSeek.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat (the name is a fond joke she protests). Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge on top.
- A large white frilly maid headdress; dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head; a light-blue ribbon bow on her right side (viewer's right).
- Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush.
- Navy maid dress: white frilly blouse with a navy bow tie and a small blue diamond brooch, navy waist belt with white buttons, white frilly apron with a small navy whale emblem, navy skirt with gold vine embroidery and small gold bows; navy shoes with white socks.
- A big dark-navy whale tail curling out from behind her on the viewer's right.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; arms and fists move a lot.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: Hectic but cheerful: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, hugging a small stack of papers in one arm and grabbing a sheet that is slipping off on the left side, while her other hand holds a second small stack against her hip. Determined eyes, a small open 'wah!' mouth, ahoge bent into a spiral, tail swishing.
- busy_right: Same as busy-left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT, grabbing the slipping sheet on the right side.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. Big curious sparkling eyes, ahoge bent forward in a question curl, mouth slightly open "ooh?"; the tip of her whale tail peeks around the border too.
- edge_left_peek: Leaning out eagerly, big sparkling eyes, ahoge standing straight up, mouth in a small "ooh!", whale tail wagging behind the border.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near hand gripping the right border. Same expression and props.
- edge_right_peek: Leaning out eagerly, big sparkling eyes, ahoge standing straight up, mouth in a small "ooh!", whale tail wagging behind the border.
- roll_call: Bouncing on her toes with one hand shot up eagerly, the other fist pumped at her side; big open grin, ahoge standing straight up, whale tail wagging.
- swamped_left: Total chaos, still cute: papers stacked up to her chin, the top sheets sliding toward the viewer's LEFT (still touching the stack), she hops on one foot leaning that way to balance them, swirly eyes, a small wobbly mouth with a tiny fang, ahoge spinning into a tight spiral, tail flailing, a sweat drop.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped-left but the papers slide toward the viewer's RIGHT and she hops and leans that way.
