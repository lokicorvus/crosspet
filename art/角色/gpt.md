## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — GPT, the white dragon girl, anime-girl personification of GPT / ChatGPT (OpenAI).
Personality: composed, elegant, mature "onee-san" (cool big-sister) aura; reliable and precise; rarely flustered, and when she is, she tries to hide it. Dry, understated humor.
Keep exactly:
- Very long, wavy, white hair with a soft lavender tint in the shadows; one curled ahoge; pointed elf-like ears.
- Two large white ridged dragon horns curving up from her head.
- Pale lavender-grey eyes with a calm, half-lidded look.
- A white interlaced-knot hair ornament on her left side (viewer's right) with a long white tassel earring.
- Small white bat-like dragon wings on her back; a long, thick white dragon tail covered in scales, curling out on the viewer's right.
- White sleeveless qipao-style dress with crystal diamond ornaments down the front, white interlaced-knot ornaments at the waist with tassels, long translucent white flowing sleeves and an open translucent white over-skirt; white shoes with knot ornaments.
Palette: almost entirely white and pale lavender; keep it soft, NEVER add strong colors.
How she shows emotion: through wings and tail (wings spread when happy or proud, droop when tired, tail curls or flicks) and very small facial changes. Even "happy" is a confident small smile, not a wide grin.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: Composed multitasking: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, tapping quickly on a slim white tablet held at chest height on that side, while her long tail curls toward the right side holding a rolled scroll. Calm focused face, one small sweat drop at her temple, wings half open, hair swaying.
- busy_right: Same as busy-left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT: now tapping the tablet on the right side, the tail holding the scroll curled toward the left.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. A calm, half-lidded sideways glance with a faint knowing smile; one wing tip and the end of her tail curl around the border next to her hand; elegant, unhurried.
- edge_left_peek: One eyebrow slightly raised, calm half-lidded eyes now open a little wider, a faint amused smile; wing tip lifted with interest, tail tip curling.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near hand gripping the right border. Same expression and props.
- edge_right_peek: One eyebrow slightly raised, calm half-lidded eyes now open a little wider, a faint amused smile; wing tip lifted with interest, tail tip curling.
- roll_call: One hand raised elegantly with relaxed fingers, the other resting at her waist; a calm, confident small smile, wings lifted a little, tail tip curling.
- swamped_left: Composure cracking: leaning toward the viewer's LEFT tapping the tablet, the tail holding a scroll, one wing curled forward pinning a second small tablet against her side, a forced elegant smile with two sweat drops, slightly wide eyes, a few hair strands out of place.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped-left but leaning toward the viewer's RIGHT, with the tablet, scroll and pinned tablet swapping sides accordingly.
