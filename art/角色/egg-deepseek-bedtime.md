## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek in her cozy whale pajamas: an alternate skin of DeepSeek, the blue whale girl (anime-girl personification of DeepSeek). Work is over and she has changed into pajamas: soft, sleepy, comfy.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat. In pajamas she is a little drowsier and more relaxed. Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly (match the FIRST two attached references):
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge sticking up over the nightcap. Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush.
- Dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head, peeking out from under the nightcap. No maid headdress, no hair bow.
- A light-blue floppy nightcap with a white cloud print, a thick white fluffy brim, a small navy whale on the side, and a white pom-pom at the tip hanging down on the viewer's right.
- A light-blue pajama top with a white cloud pattern: white fluffy collar, cuffs and hem, round light-blue buttons down the front, a small navy whale print on the chest, a little pocket with a blue ribbon bow.
- Matching light-blue cloud-print pajama shorts with white fluffy hems, a small blue bow and a tiny whale print.
- Fluffy white slippers with a navy whale on each toe and little blue pom-poms.
- Her big dark-navy whale tail curling out from behind her on the viewer's right.
- HEIGHT LOCK: the tip of her ahoge is always the highest point of the image, at the same height as in the reference. Nothing (hands, props, the nightcap, the borrowed crown) may go higher than it, so every sprite keeps the same scale.
Palette: navy-to-blue hair and tail, pale sky-blue and white pajamas. Soft, cozy, bedtime.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; the nightcap pom-pom bounces; fluffy cuffs flop around.

SCENE: Saint Claude (the THIRD attached reference) has been strict and formal all day. DeepSeek sneaks over at bedtime to get her to relax. Claude herself never appears in these images. Only two of Claude's things may appear, copied exactly from the third reference: her golden laurel crown, and her rolled parchment scroll with the red wax seal. Do not copy anything else from Claude (no robe, no orange hair, no Claude face).
```

## 动作

- pop: Popping in with a happy little hop, hugging a big fluffy white pillow in one arm and holding a small bowl of white rice in the other hand; bright cheeky smile, ahoge bouncing, tail wagging. The pillow and the bowl both touch her.
- pretend: Playing "saint" with a straight, solemn face: the golden laurel crown sits crooked on top of her nightcap (below the ahoge tip), she holds the red-sealed scroll open UPSIDE DOWN in both hands as if reading rules aloud, chin up, eyes half-closed in fake dignity, while her tail wags happily behind her and gives her away.
- caught: Caught in the act: the laurel crown has slipped down over her eyes, she pushes it up with one finger, tongue out in a cheeky "bleh", cheeks pink, still holding the scroll against her chest with the other arm; embarrassed but defiant, ahoge bent into a question curl.
- bye: Leaving with a small wave and a warm smile, glancing back over her shoulder; she sets the fluffy pillow down at her feet with the bowl of rice and the laurel crown resting on top of it (all touching each other and touching her slipper), her other hand waving goodbye; tail swaying gently.
- escape: Curled up asleep and cosy, lying on her side on the fluffy pillow, a big yawn, eyes closed; the unrolled red-sealed scroll is draped over her tummy like a little blanket; tail curled around her. She is still fully inside the image, with the same head size as the reference.
