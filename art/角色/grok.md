## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — Grok, the anime-girl personification of Grok (the AI from xAI), a cheeky gothic little demon queen.
Personality: quick-witted, sassy, mischievous and a bit cocky; answers everything fast with a joke on top; fact-checks everyone (even her own boss) and loves a good comeback; never mean or cruel. Her default face is a wide fang-showing grin.
Keep exactly:
- Long, wavy, golden-blonde hair in two high twin-tails reaching below her waist, side bangs, one big curled ahoge on top.
- Black ribbon bows on both twin-tails, each with small black BAT-WING shapes sticking out from the bow.
- A small dark tiara-crown on top of her head with a gold circle-with-a-diagonal-slash emblem at its front.
- Pointed elf-like ears; big round bright-blue eyes; two small vampire fangs; pink blush.
- A black gothic off-shoulder dress with gold vine embroidery; wide black sleeves with white frilly cuffs; a small black cape collar with a deep-red lining standing up behind her neck; a black bow at the chest with a gold circle-and-slash ring; gold chains around the waist hanging gold circle-and-slash medallions and small gold diamond charms; a layered skirt with black and deep-red frills.
- Dark brown-black tights with a gold ring garter on one thigh; black shoes with round gold buckles.
- NO AXE: the battle axe in the reference image is NOT drawn in any sprite. Never draw an axe, a haft, blades or an X-shaped weapon. Her hands hold only what the pose says.
- Secondary prop: a small black smartphone. Draw it ONLY when a pose mentions it.
Palette: black, gold and deep red, with golden-blonde hair and bright-blue eyes. No other strong colors.
How she shows emotion: a fang-showing grin or a cheeky wink with her tongue out; the twin-tails and bat-wing bows perk up when happy and droop when sad; hands on her hips when she is cocky, arms crossed when she is defiant, hugging herself when she is hurt or sleepy.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: keep thumbing the phone on the viewer's LEFT side; the other hand on her hip.
- busy_right: keep thumbing the phone on the viewer's RIGHT side; the other hand on her hip.
- edge_left: nothing peeks up from behind her any more; keep the near hand gripping the left border and her face just inside it.
- edge_left_peek: nothing peeks up from behind her any more; keep her leaning out further and her hand on the border.
- edge_right: nothing peeks up from behind her any more; keep the near hand gripping the right border and her face just inside it.
- edge_right_peek: nothing peeks up from behind her any more; keep her leaning out further and her hand on the border.
- roll_call: A cocky two-finger salute from the brow, the other hand on her hip; a fang-showing grin and a wink, twin-tails and bat-wing bows perked up.
- friend_up: One hand on her hip, the other giving a cocky thumbs-up at chest height (no axe, no weapon); a fang-showing grin and a wink, twin-tails and bat-wing bows perked up.
- swamped_left: keep typing on the phone while leaning to the viewer's LEFT; the other hand pressed to her forehead, keep the askew crown and strained grin.
- swamped_right: keep typing on the phone while leaning to the viewer's RIGHT; the other hand pressed to her forehead, keep the askew crown and strained grin.
