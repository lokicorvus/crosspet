参考图: art/原图/kimi/idle/v1.png

故事：Kimi 是「月之暗面」，一直想看看自己的家（月亮背面）长什么样。趁群星不在，她溜进观星台借望远镜看月亮，结果从地球上永远只看得到月亮正面。
被你抓到时眼睛周围还印着一圈目镜压出来的红印；没被发现的话，她就靠着望远镜给月亮拉一首小夜曲。
群星本人不出场，只出现她的黄铜望远镜（三脚架）和星图卷轴。

## 动作

- pop: Tiptoeing in on the balls of her feet, glancing to one side with bright curious eyes, one index finger pressed to her lips in a "shh", the other hand carrying her small closed navy violin case by its handle; a giddy, sneaky little smile, hair and crystal swinging.
- pretend: Standing on tiptoe at Astra's telescope, which stands on its tripod right beside her on the viewer's left and points up toward the viewer's upper left. She presses HER RIGHT eye (viewer's left) to the eyepiece, the other eye squeezed shut, both hands holding the brass tube; her mouth is a small eager "ooh" and her cheeks are pink with excitement. The telescope's front end stays below the top of her headdress.
- caught: Caught: she has just jerked back from the telescope, which still stands beside her on the viewer's left with one of her hands on its tripod leg; the other hand is raised near her chest. A faint round pink ring is pressed around HER RIGHT eye from the eyepiece. Both eyes wide open, a small startled open mouth, a sweat drop on her cheek, shoulders up. A flustered, guilty, very cute expression.
- bye: Leaving with a sheepish smile and a small wave, glancing back over her shoulder; her other hand holds out Astra's rolled navy star chart with a single loose sheet of her own sheet music tucked into it, as if returning it with a thank-you note. The faint pink eyepiece ring is still around HER RIGHT eye.
- escape: Unnoticed and content: sitting on the ground with her legs folded to one side, leaning her back lightly against one leg of Astra's telescope tripod (the telescope stands behind her, touching her), playing her small warm-wood violin with a short bow, eyes softly closed, a dreamy little smile, as if playing a lullaby to the moon. The violin stays below her face; both bow ends inside the picture. She stays fully inside the image with the same head size as the reference. No floating notes.
