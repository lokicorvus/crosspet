## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — Kimi, the anime-girl personification of Kimi (the AI from Moonshot AI, "the dark side of the moon"), a moonlit little maid who loves music.
Personality (draft): bright, chatty and eager; a little dreamy, hums tunes while she works. Never cold or mean. Her default face is a big open-mouthed happy smile.
Keep exactly:
- Very long, wavy, silver-white hair that fades to pale icy blue toward the tips; side bangs falling between big eyes.
- Big round violet-purple eyes, pink blush.
- A white frilly maid headdress on a navy hairband; on HER LEFT side (viewer's right): a silver crescent-moon hair ornament, a small silver music-note pin, and a navy polka-dot ribbon bow with a silver "K" charm, navy-and-white striped ribbon tails, silver chains and a small clear diamond-shaped crystal hanging down.
- Navy-and-white maid dress: white frilly blouse front with a navy bow at the collar holding a round "K" badge, small navy buttons; puffy navy sleeves with white frilly cuffs; a navy waistband with small gold buttons and a thin silver chain; a white frilly apron with a silver treble-clef charm on one side and a silver "K" charm on the other, a faint music-staff embroidery across it, and a navy bow with a small silver crescent moon on its lower corner; a navy skirt with a faint gold starry / constellation pattern and white frilly hems.
- White frilly ankle socks, navy Mary-Jane shoes with small bows.
The "K" charms are part of her design: keep them exactly, but do NOT add any other text or letters.
Palette: navy blue, white and silver, with silver-white-to-icy-blue hair and violet eyes. No other strong colors.
Musical identity: her chosen instrument is a violin, included only when the pose calls for it. A small natural warm-wood violin is allowed as a prop; preserve all her original outfit colors. Unbound loose sheet music or a single manuscript sheet can carry her musical identity in reading/writing poses without adding a violin. Sheet music must not be depicted as a bound book, notebook, hard cover or thick page block. DeepSeek's corresponding sprites may inform natural expression and pose, while Kimi's own approved reference always governs her identity, costume and art style.
How she shows emotion: big expressive mouth and eyes, bouncing on her toes, hands clasped together in front of her chest, the long hair and the hanging crystal swinging.
```
