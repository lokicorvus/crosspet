# Gemini

参考图: characters/gemini/idle.webp, docs/prompts/ref-gemini.webp, characters/gemini/portrait.webp

## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — Gemini, the starry cat girl, anime-girl personification of Gemini (Google).
Personality: bright, playful, mischievous, curious and quick; a little smug (the ":3" cat smile); the "twin" theme shows as two-sided moods, from teasing to sweet.
Keep exactly:
- Long voluminous wavy hair, blue-violet at the top fading to pink-magenta at the tips; one curled ahoge; two small side buns.
- Blue-violet cat ears with pink inner fur and white fluff.
- Heterochromia: HER RIGHT eye (viewer's left) is pink-magenta, HER LEFT eye (viewer's right) is amber-gold. Keep this exactly.
- A large iridescent four-pointed star hairclip with gold rim on her left side (viewer's right) with small dangling star charms; small star earrings.
- Navy crop-top with a high collar and star gems, white off-shoulder sleeves with navy cuffs, a violet sash, a navy skirt over a white frilly petticoat, iridescent four-pointed star charms hanging from the outfit, white leg warmers, navy-violet platform shoes.
- A fluffy blue-violet cat tail on the viewer's right.
How she shows emotion: cat body language. Ears perk up or flatten, tail puffs, curls or swishes; cat-like poses (paw hands, stretching like a cat). The ":3" smile is her default.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: Cat multitasking: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, holding a small open laptop against her hip on that side and tapping it with one paw-hand, while her fluffy tail curls toward the right side batting at the trailing cable. Ears turned in two directions, a busy but smug ':3', tail fur a little fluffed.
- busy_right: Same as busy-left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT, laptop and tapping on the right, tail batting the cable on the left.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. Peeking like a cat: ears perked forward, a paw-hand resting on the border, a playful ":3" face; the fluffy tail curls up around the border.
- edge_left_peek: Leaning out like a curious cat, ears perked straight forward, head tilted, a paw-hand raised as if about to bat at the cursor, ":3" mouth, tail curled up.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near hand gripping the right border. Same expression and props.
- edge_right_peek: Leaning out like a curious cat, ears perked straight forward, head tilted, a paw-hand raised as if about to bat at the cursor, ":3" mouth, tail curled up.
- roll_call: A paw-hand raised beside her head like a beckoning cat, the other paw at her chest; ":3" mouth open in a cheerful call, ears perked straight up, tail curled.
- friend_up: One paw-hand raised beside her cheek in a beckoning-cat wave, the other paw at her chest; ears tilted forward, a delighted ":3" smile with a light blush, fluffy tail curled into a hook.
- swamped_left: Overwhelmed cat: laptop tucked under one arm, tapping it with the paw-hand, the tail curled up holding a small pencil as a third hand, leaning toward the viewer's LEFT on one foot, fur and tail puffed up, ears flattened back, round surprised eyes, a small wobbly ':3', one sweat drop.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped-left but leaning toward the viewer's RIGHT with the tasks swapping sides.
