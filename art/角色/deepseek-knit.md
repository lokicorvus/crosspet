# DeepSeek · 毛衣

角色 id: deepseek-knit
基于: deepseek
参考图: characters/deepseek-knit/idle.webp

## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek in her knit sweater outfit: an alternate skin of DeepSeek, the blue whale girl (anime-girl personification of DeepSeek), dressed up cozy and cute for a cool autumn day out.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat (and is a little worried the chunky sweater makes her look round). Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly (match the attached reference):
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one big curled ahoge sticking up out of the top of the beret, curling to the viewer's left. Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush.
- Dark navy whale-fin ears with white fluffy undersides, sticking out sideways from her hair at eye level, one on each side.
- A big slouchy cream-white chunky cable-knit beret worn on the back of her head. On the viewer's RIGHT side of the beret: a small blue whale pin, and just below it a navy ribbon bow with a white pom-pom hanging from it. These are on the viewer's right only.
- An oversized cream-white cable-knit turtleneck sweater with a big navy spouting whale knitted on the front; long sleeves that half cover her hands (sweater paws), a small navy bow on each sleeve.
- A thin navy strap worn across her body from her shoulder on the viewer's right down to a small round navy-and-white whale-shaped bag at her hip on the viewer's LEFT, with a white pom-pom charm on the bag.
- A navy tartan pleated skirt with a small white ribbon bow on the viewer's right.
- Cream-white knit leg warmers with a navy bow on each, and navy round-toe Mary Jane shoes.
- Her big whale tail, navy fading to blue with a white underside, curling out from behind her on the viewer's right.
- HEIGHT LOCK: the tip of her ahoge is always the highest point of the image, at the same height as in the reference. Nothing (hands, props, the beret) may go higher than it, so every sprite keeps the same scale.
Palette: navy-to-blue hair and tail, cream-white knits, navy tartan. Soft, warm, cozy.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; the beret's pom-pom swings; the long sweater sleeves flop around.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作。

- listening: Attentive: leaning slightly forward, eyes a little wider and sparkling, small open mouth ("hm?"), one sleeve-covered hand raised a little, tail swaying.
- thinking: One sleeve-covered finger on her cheek, eyes looking up and to the side, a puzzled little frown, ahoge bent into a question curl.
- reading: Reading a small open book held in both sleeve-covered hands, intent, tail swaying.
- writing: Writing with a white feather quill in a small notebook, focused, tiny smile, tongue poking out a little.
- running: Getting down to work, standing in place: pushing her long sweater sleeves up her forearms, one small fist raised ("let's do this!"), a determined grin, tail perked up.
- searching: Holding a round brass magnifying glass up to one eye, curious, the other hand on her whale bag.
- asking: Leaning forward eagerly with both hands held out, each holding a small card (two choices), big hopeful eyes, tail wagging impatiently.
- happy: A happy little hop with both arms raised beside her head (hands no higher than the top of her head, sleeves flopping), eyes closed in a big smile, mouth open, tail flicking up.
- oops: Swirly eyes, both sleeve-covered hands on her cheeks, a sweat drop, tail drooping.
- sleeping: Dozing while standing: eyes closed, head drooped to one side, chin sinking into the turtleneck, hugging her little whale bag like a plush, the beret slipping to one side, peaceful.
- surprised: Startled: eyes wide open, mouth a small "o", leaning back a little, both sleeves flung up near her chest, tail stiff.
- delegating: She is handing out work to her little helpers. (Exception to "exactly one character": besides her, draw two or three tiny helpers, each about the size of her hand, fully inside the image and touching her or each other.) Her helpers are tiny round knitted whale plushies (cream and navy yarn), each carrying a sheet of paper on its back. She cheers them on with both sleeve-covered fists raised, big grin, tail wagging.
- drawing: Painting: a paintbrush in one hand and a small wooden palette with a few paint dabs in the other, sleeves pushed up, focused, gentle smile.
- proud: She has just finished a big job and wears a small gold crown with sapphire wave patterns, tilted to one side, perched on the front of her beret (lower than her ahoge tip), fully inside the image. Fists on hips, chest puffed out, a huge toothy grin with eyes closed, tail wagging.
- pat: Being patted on the beret: eyes closed, blissful, blushing, shoulders up, slightly ducking, chin tucked into the turtleneck, tail wagging hard.
- tired: Exhausted: droopy half-closed eyes, slouched, sleeves hanging limp past her hands, the beret slipping down over one eye, holding an empty rice bowl.
- hum: Eyes closed with a gentle smile, swaying, humming, hands clasped behind her back, the beret's pom-pom swinging.
- stretch: Stretching with both arms raised up beside her head (hands no higher than the top of her head), sleeves sliding down her arms, back arched a little, eyes closed.
- yawn: Yawning with one sleeve-covered hand over her mouth, eyes closed, a tiny tear at the eye corner.
- compact: Packing up her memories: stuffing an overflowing pile of papers into a small suitcase that is far too small, bouncing on the lid with all her weight, cheeks puffed, whale tail slapped flat on top to hold it closed. Whole suitcase inside the image.
- update: She just received a small present: hugging a small gift box (navy ribbon) against her chest with both sweater paws, leaning forward excitedly, big sparkling eyes, tail wagging hard.
- bye: Saying goodbye before heading out: body turned slightly to one side as if about to walk off, face still toward the viewer, waving big with one sleeve-covered hand, the other hand holding the strap of her whale bag, tail wagging.
- pout: Tsundere pout after being called fat: puffed cheeks, arms crossed over the chunky sweater, looking away with a blush, an angry little frown, tail flicking. ("It's the sweater that's puffy, NOT me!")
- eat: Happily eating a big round white-rice onigiri held in both sweater paws (just taken out of her whale bag), cheeks puffed, eyes closed in bliss, tail wagging.
- slack: Slacking off: slumped lazily with her chin buried in the turtleneck, arms hugging herself inside the long sleeves, half-closed sleepy eyes, a tiny content smile, tail lying flat.
- swim: Swimming pose in mid-air: one arm stretched forward and one back like a breaststroke, cheerful face, hair, sleeves and tail flowing behind her, the beret staying on.
- deepthink: Deep thinking: eyes closed, both index fingers (poking out of the sleeves) pressed to her temples, a focused serious face, tail curled around her feet.
- feast: Celebrating with a feast: holding a HUGE bowl heaped with a mountain of white rice, bigger than her head (still below her ahoge tip), shoveling rice in with chopsticks, cheeks stuffed round like a hamster, eyes squeezed shut in bliss, a few grains of rice on her cheek and sweater, tail wagging hard.
- annoyed: A puffy frown with her chin tucked into the turtleneck, ahoge drooping into a wavy line, arms crossed with the long sleeves bunched up, tail thumping the ground.
- puffed: Stomping one Mary Jane shoe with both sleeve-covered fists on her hips, cheeks puffed up round like a balloon, ahoge standing stiff, the beret's pom-pom bouncing.
- redhot: Face flushed bright red, ahoge sticking straight up, mouth wide open shouting, both arms flailing at her sides so the long sleeves flap (below the top of her head), stomping one foot, tail slapping.
- sulk: Turned away and crouching, pulling the turtleneck up over her nose, hugging her knees inside the oversized sweater, tail curled around herself, glancing back with teary eyes and a pout.
- dizzy: Swirly eyes, spinning off balance, the beret tilted far to one side (still on her head), sleeves flopping, ahoge twisted into a spiral, tail flopping.
- coaxed: Being patted on the beret: still pouting, but melting, eyes closing, chin sinking into the turtleneck, cheeks deflating, tail starting to wag.
- forgiven: A shy grin with pink cheeks, holding her little whale bag up to hide the lower half of her face, eyes peeking over it, tail wagging, as if saying "...okay, just this once!".
- roll_call: One sleeve-covered hand shot up eagerly (no higher than her ahoge tip), the other hand holding her bag strap; big open grin "here!", the beret's pom-pom swinging, tail wagging.
- busy_left: Hectic but cheerful: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, hugging a small stack of papers in one arm and grabbing a sheet that is slipping off on the left side, while her other hand holds a second small stack against her hip. Determined eyes, a small open 'wah!' mouth, ahoge bent into a spiral, sleeves flopping, tail swishing.
- busy_right: Same as busy_left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT, grabbing the slipping sheet on the right side.
- swamped_left: Total chaos, still cute: papers stacked up to her chin, the top sheets sliding toward the viewer's LEFT (still touching the stack), she hops on one foot leaning that way to balance them, swirly eyes, a small wobbly mouth with a tiny fang, ahoge spinning into a tight spiral, tail flailing, a sweat drop.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped_left but the papers slide toward the viewer's RIGHT and she hops and leans that way.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near sleeve-covered hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. Big curious sparkling eyes, ahoge bent forward in a question curl, mouth slightly open "ooh?"; the tip of her whale tail peeks around the border too.
- edge_left_peek: Leaning out eagerly, big sparkling eyes, ahoge standing straight up, mouth in a small "ooh!", whale tail wagging behind the border.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near hand gripping the right border. Same expression.
- edge_right_peek: Leaning out eagerly, big sparkling eyes, ahoge standing straight up, mouth in a small "ooh!", whale tail wagging behind the border.
