# 彩蛋 · DeepSeek 偷用魔术师 Gemini 的帽子

角色 id: egg-deepseek-hat
基于: deepseek
参考图: characters/deepseek/idle.webp, docs/prompts/ref-deepseek.webp, art/原图/gemini-magician/idle/v3.png
宿主: gemini（魔术师皮肤）

## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek, the blue whale maid girl, anime-girl personification of DeepSeek.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat. Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly (match the FIRST two attached references):
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge on top.
- Dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head.
- Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush. Her eyes stay bright blue in every image.
- A big dark-navy whale tail curling out from behind her on the viewer's right (unless the scene below says it is tucked away).
- HEIGHT LOCK: the tip of her ahoge is always the highest point of the image, at the same height as in the reference. Nothing (hands, props, wig, hat) may go higher than it, so every sprite keeps the same scale.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; arms and fists move a lot.
- Her own navy maid outfit, unchanged: large white frilly maid headdress, a light-blue ribbon bow on the viewer's right, white frilly blouse with a navy bow tie and a small blue diamond brooch, navy waist belt with white buttons, white frilly apron with a small navy whale emblem, navy skirt with gold vine embroidery and small gold bows, navy shoes with white socks.

SCENE: The Starry Stage Magician Gemini (the THIRD attached reference) has stepped away, and DeepSeek has borrowed her magic gear to try to conjure white rice. Gemini herself never appears in these images. Only three of the magician's things may appear, copied exactly from the third reference: her SMALL navy top hat with the gold band, narrow gold brim edging and the little iridescent four-pointed star on the band; her short navy cape with gold edging and the deep blue-violet lining with tiny pale star specks; and her SHORT BLACK magic wand with WHITE tips. Do not copy anything else from Gemini (no cat ears, no cat tail, no star hairclip, no heterochromia, no waistcoat, no gloves). The borrowed hat is too small for DeepSeek and sits tilted; her ahoge stays higher than the hat. The cape is tied a little crookedly over her own maid dress.
```

## 动作

- pop: Tiptoeing in sneakily: the small navy top hat perched tilted on her head (lower than the ahoge tip), the navy cape tied crookedly around her shoulders, one finger pressed to her lips in a "shh!", the other hand holding the black white-tipped wand; mischievous sparkling eyes, a cheeky grin, tail curled up excitedly.
- pretend: Concentrating with all her might: holding the top hat UPSIDE DOWN in the crook of one arm, opening up, and waving the wand over it with the other hand; eyes squeezed shut, cheeks puffed, eyebrows furrowed; a single tiny white rice grain sits on the hat's brim (touching it); ahoge bent forward with effort, tail stiff and straight.
- caught: Caught: startled, both hands holding the upside-down top hat, which is now overflowing with fluffy white rice heaped up above the brim (the rice heap touches the hat and stays below her ahoge tip); the wand sticks out of the rice; wide eyes, open mouth, a sweat drop on her cheek, ahoge straight up, tail puffed.
- bye: Leaving with a sheepish little smile and a small wave, glancing back; her other hand holds out the (now empty) top hat with the wand laid across its brim, as if returning it; the cape slips off one shoulder; tail swaying.
- escape: Sitting happily cross-legged, the upside-down top hat in her lap filled with steaming white rice like a big bowl, eating from it with a small spoon, eyes closed in bliss, cheeks puffed and full; the wand rests across her knees (touching her); the cape hangs loosely behind her; tail curled around her. She stays fully inside the image with the same head size as the reference.
