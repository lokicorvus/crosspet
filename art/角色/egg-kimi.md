# 彩蛋 · DeepSeek 装成 Kimi（半吊子变装）

角色 id: egg-kimi
基于: deepseek
参考图: characters/deepseek/idle.webp, docs/prompts/ref-deepseek.webp, characters/kimi/idle.webp
宿主: kimi

> 2026-11-17 重新设计（loki 选「半吊子变装」）：第一版让她戴银白假发、穿 Kimi 整套衣服、尾巴收进裙子，两人本来都是藏青女仆装，戴上假发后和 Kimi 几乎分不出来（pop v1–v3）。
> 现在不戴假发、不换衣服：深蓝头发和鲸鱼尾巴一直露着，一眼看得出是 DeepSeek；只借 Kimi 最好认的几样（小提琴、银月牙发饰、带 K 的蝴蝶结），再加一个她自己用纸板剪的月亮。笑点是「她觉得自己很像」。

## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek, the blue whale maid girl, anime-girl personification of DeepSeek.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat. Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly (match the FIRST two attached references):
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge on top.
- Dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head.
- Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush. Her eyes stay bright blue in every image.
- Her OWN outfit, unchanged from the first reference: white frilly maid headdress with her light-blue ribbon bow, white frilly blouse with a navy bow tie and a small blue diamond brooch, navy waist belt with white buttons, white frilly apron with the small navy WHALE emblem, navy skirt with gold vine embroidery and small gold bows, navy shoes with white socks.
- Her big dark-navy whale tail is fully out, curling from behind her on the viewer's right, in every image.
- HEIGHT LOCK: the tip of her ahoge is always the highest point of the image, at the same height as in the reference. Nothing (hands, props, the cardboard moon) may go higher than it, so every sprite keeps the same scale.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; arms and fists move a lot.

SCENE: DeepSeek is pretending to be Kimi (the THIRD attached reference), the moonlit music maid, and is very proud of her terrible disguise. Kimi herself never appears. The joke is that she is OBVIOUSLY still DeepSeek: she must read as DeepSeek at a glance. NO wig, NO silver-white or icy-blue hair anywhere, NO violet eyes, NO Kimi dress or apron, NO "K" badge on her collar: all of her navy-blue hair, her own whale-emblem apron and her whale tail stay fully visible.
She has borrowed only a few of Kimi's things, copied from the third reference:
- Kimi's silver crescent-moon hair ornament and Kimi's navy polka-dot ribbon bow with the silver "K" charm, clipped a little crookedly onto her own headdress on the viewer's LEFT side (her own light-blue bow stays on the right).
- Kimi's small warm-wood violin (and its bow, when the pose says so).
Plus her own homemade prop: a flat crescent moon cut out of plain tan cardboard with rough uneven edges, scribbled over in pale-yellow crayon, taped onto a wooden chopstick. It is clearly homemade and a bit lopsided. When she is not holding it, it is tucked into her waist belt (touching her).
The "K" charm on the borrowed bow is part of Kimi's design: keep it, but add no other text or letters.
```

## 动作

- pop: Popping in with a proud little hop, "ta-da!": one hand holds up the cardboard-moon chopstick beside her face (the moon stays below her ahoge tip), the other arm hugs Kimi's violin UPSIDE DOWN against her side (scroll pointing down, bow tucked under the same arm); big confident grin, ahoge bouncing, whale tail curled up excitedly.
- pretend: Trying very hard to look like a graceful musician: the violin tucked under her chin, eyes closed in exaggerated fake bliss, eyebrows raised, mouth in a small proud "o", bowing the strings with a wooden rice paddle (shamoji) instead of the violin bow; the cardboard moon tucked into her waist belt; whale tail wagging.
- caught: Caught: one violin string has snapped and curls loose from the violin (still attached to it); the borrowed crescent-moon clip has slipped down crookedly over her navy bangs; the cardboard moon has dropped and leans bent against her shoe (touching it); flustered open-mouthed face, a sweat drop on her cheek, ahoge bent into a question curl, hugging the violin to her chest with both arms; whale tail sticking straight up in surprise.
- bye: Leaving with a sheepish grin and a small wave, glancing back over her shoulder; her other hand holds out Kimi's crescent-moon clip and K bow on her open palm, giving them back; Kimi's violin stands leaning against her shoe (touching it), left behind for its owner; the cardboard moon is still tucked in her belt, she is keeping that one; whale tail swaying.
- escape: Skipping away happily, unnoticed: the borrowed moon clip and K bow still on her headdress, violin tucked under one arm, taking a big bite from an onigiri held in the other hand, eyes closed in a blissful smile, cheeks puffed; the cardboard moon tucked in her belt; whale tail flicking happily. Same head size as the reference.
