## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek in her cozy whale pajamas: an alternate skin of DeepSeek, the blue whale girl (anime-girl personification of DeepSeek). Work is over and she has changed into pajamas: soft, sleepy, comfy.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat. In pajamas she is a little drowsier and more relaxed. Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly (match the attached reference):
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge sticking up over the nightcap. Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush.
- Dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head, peeking out from under the nightcap. No maid headdress, no hair bow.
- A light-blue floppy nightcap with a white cloud print, a thick white fluffy brim, a small navy whale on the side, and a white pom-pom at the tip hanging down on the viewer's right.
- A light-blue pajama top with a white cloud pattern: white fluffy collar, cuffs and hem, round light-blue buttons down the front, a small navy whale print on the chest, a little pocket with a blue ribbon bow.
- Matching light-blue cloud-print pajama shorts with white fluffy hems, a small blue bow and a tiny whale print.
- Fluffy white slippers with a navy whale on each toe and little blue pom-poms.
- Her big dark-navy whale tail curling out from behind her on the viewer's right.
- HEIGHT LOCK: the tip of her ahoge is always the highest point of the image, at the same height as in the reference. Nothing (hands, props, the nightcap) may go higher than it, so every sprite keeps the same scale.
Palette: navy-to-blue hair and tail, pale sky-blue and white pajamas. Soft, cozy, bedtime.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; the nightcap pom-pom bounces; fluffy cuffs flop around.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: Hectic but cheerful: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, hugging a small stack of papers in one arm and grabbing a sheet that is slipping off on the left side, while her other hand holds a second small stack against her hip. Determined eyes, a small open 'wah!' mouth, ahoge bent into a spiral, tail swishing.
- busy_right: Same as busy-left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT, grabbing the slipping sheet on the right side.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. Big curious sparkling eyes, ahoge bent forward in a question curl, mouth slightly open "ooh?"; the tip of her whale tail peeks around the border too.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near hand gripping the right border. Same expression and props.
- roll_call: One fluffy cuff raised a little slowly, the other hand rubbing one sleepy eye; a drowsy but happy open-mouthed "here~", nightcap pom-pom swinging.
- swamped_left: Total chaos, still cute: papers stacked up to her chin, the top sheets sliding toward the viewer's LEFT (still touching the stack), she hops on one foot leaning that way to balance them, swirly eyes, a small wobbly mouth with a tiny fang, ahoge spinning into a tight spiral, tail flailing, a sweat drop.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped-left but the papers slide toward the viewer's RIGHT and she hops and leans that way.
