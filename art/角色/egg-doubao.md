## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek, the blue whale maid girl, anime-girl personification of DeepSeek.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice. Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly (match the FIRST two attached references):
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge on top.
- Dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head.
- Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush. Her eyes stay bright blue whenever her face shows.
- Her OWN outfit, unchanged from the first reference: white frilly maid headdress with her light-blue ribbon bow, white frilly blouse with a navy bow tie and a small blue diamond brooch, navy waist belt with white buttons, white frilly apron with the small navy WHALE emblem, navy skirt with gold vine embroidery and small gold bows, navy shoes with white socks.
- Her big dark-navy whale tail is fully out, curling from behind her on the viewer's right, in every image.
- Same chibi proportions as the first reference (about 2.5 heads tall).
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; arms and fists move a lot.

SCENE: DeepSeek is pretending to be Doubao (the THIRD attached reference, the cheerful everyday-helper girl with lavender-to-aqua hair) by wearing Doubao's signature prop, and she is very proud of her terrible disguise. Doubao herself never appears. The joke is that she is OBVIOUSLY still DeepSeek: NO lavender or aqua hair, NO side ponytail, NO Doubao outfit, NO lanyard or badge, NO violet eyes. Her own maid outfit, her navy-blue hair and her whale tail stay fully visible.
THE BORROWED PROP — Doubao's MASCOT HEAD, copied exactly from the third reference: a soft plush costume head in the style of the Doubao app icon: a round pale face with a brown bob haircut, small round black eyes, tiny eyebrows and a small round "o" mouth. It is a soft prop, not a character: its face never changes expression. It is a little too big for DeepSeek.
- When she WEARS it, it covers her whole head down to the chin. Her long navy-blue wavy hair spills out from under its lower edge down her back and shoulders, and her two navy whale-fin ears poke out sideways through its sides, so she is still recognizable. Her maid headdress is hidden under it.
- HEIGHT LOCK: the top of the worn mascot head is the highest point of the image, at the same height as the tip of her ahoge in the first reference. When she is not wearing it, the tip of her ahoge is the highest point at that same height. Nothing (hands, the mascot head) may go higher, so every sprite keeps the same scale.
- No text, letters or numbers anywhere.
```

## 动作

- pop: Popping in with a proud little hop, "ta-da!", WEARING the mascot head: both arms flung wide open in a big cheerful welcome (copying Doubao's bouncy greeting), one foot kicked up behind; her long navy hair bouncing out from under the mascot head, whale-fin ears sticking out its sides, whale tail curled up excitedly behind her.
- pretend: Trying very hard to act like Doubao, still WEARING the mascot head: bouncing on her toes with both fists raised cheerfully at shoulder height, as if saying "I'm Doubao, ask me anything!"; the mascot head's blank face makes it extra silly. Her whale tail is wagging so hard it swings out wide behind her on the viewer's right, a dead giveaway; navy hair spilling out below the head.
- caught: Caught: the mascot head has slipped and tilted sideways, pushed up crookedly on top of her head (still on her, the top no higher than the HEIGHT LOCK), so her own face shows: wide bright-blue eyes, a flustered open-mouthed "eep!", a sweat drop on her cheek, blush; both hands grabbing the slipping head; her maid headdress is squashed underneath; whale tail sticking straight up in surprise.
- bye: Not wearing it any more: holding the mascot head out in front of her with both hands at chest height, giving it back, with a sheepish apologetic grin and one eye closed; hair a little messy with the ahoge sticking up, maid headdress slightly crooked; whale tail swaying slowly.
- escape: Skipping away happily, unnoticed, still WEARING the mascot head: body turned toward the viewer's right mid-skip, one hand holding a small onigiri, the other arm swinging; navy hair streaming out behind from under the head; whale tail flicking happily. Same scale as the reference.
