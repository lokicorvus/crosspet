## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — GPT (the white dragon girl, anime-girl personification of GPT) in her "dragon-cub onesie" skin, an alternate outfit. In this outfit she is a sweet, slightly airheaded version of herself: gentle, a half-beat slow to react, absent-minded and curious, quietly over-careful. Soft and cute, NEVER dumb or mocked: no drool, no crossed eyes, no blank or dead eyes, no silly faces.
Keep exactly:
- Very long, wavy WHITE hair (soft lavender tint only in the shadows) flowing out from under the hood over her shoulders; one curled ahoge poking out through the top of the hood.
- Big round pale-lavender eyes with soft round highlights; soft pink blush.
- An oversized lavender dragon-cub onesie with dashed stitch lines. The hood is always up, with two cream stuffed horns (the one on the viewer's left flops over slightly). The long sleeves hide her hands ("sleeve-paws"); when she holds something, she grips it with the sleeve ends or only her fingertips peek out.
- A cream belly with stitched scale lines, and on its center a handmade white felt knot patch (six interlaced rounded loops, lavender running stitches around it, one short loose thread dangling below). Whenever her front is visible, this patch must be visible and recognizable.
- Round cream footies with stitched claws; small lavender plush wings with cream undersides on her back; a fat stuffed lavender tail with cream spikes curling out on the viewer's right.
- Her real horns, elf ears and dress are NOT drawn. NO other knot ornaments, NO tassels, NO hair accessories.
Palette: lavender onesie, cream belly / horns / paws / spikes, white hair, pale-pink blush. Keep it soft; never add strong colors.
How she shows emotion: soft and small. Head tilts, the ahoge bends, the floppy stuffed horn droops or perks up, long sleeves flop, plush wings flutter or droop, the tail sways. Even big emotions stay gentle: a small hop, a little "o" mouth, a closed-eye smile. No wide grins, no shouting.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: Carefully multitasking: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, tapping a slim white tablet held at chest height on that side with one sleeve-paw, while her stuffed tail curls toward the right side holding a small rolled scroll. A focused, slightly puzzled face, one small sweat drop on her cheek, the ahoge bent.
- busy_right: Same as busy_left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT: now tapping the tablet on the right side, the tail holding the scroll curled toward the left.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near sleeve-paw are just inside it, the sleeve-paw gripping the left border. A curious, slightly sleepy sideways glance with a small "hm?" mouth; one plush wing tip and the end of her stuffed tail curl around the border next to her sleeve.
- edge_left_peek: One sleeve-paw on the border, the other sleeve-paw lifted shyly to her cheek; a slow, sleepy-curious little "o" mouth, the ahoge perking up, the floppy stuffed horn bouncing.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near sleeve-paw gripping the right border. Same expression and props.
- edge_right_peek: One sleeve-paw on the border, the other sleeve-paw lifted shyly to her cheek; a slow, sleepy-curious little "o" mouth, the ahoge perking up, the floppy stuffed horn bouncing.
- roll_call: One sleeve-paw raised a half-beat late, the other sleeve-paw at her belly patch; a sleepy, earnest little "o" mouth turning into a soft smile, the ahoge perking up.
- friend_up: Both long sleeve-paws pressed over her mouth, giggling quietly behind them; eyes curved in a soft smile, the hood tilted a little so the floppy stuffed horn droops further, ahoge perked up, plush wings fluttering.
- swamped_left: Getting overwhelmed: leaning toward the viewer's LEFT tapping the white tablet, the tail holding a scroll, and a second small white tablet balanced on top of her hood between the stuffed horns, tilting and about to slide off; head tilted as she tries to keep it balanced, eyes glancing up at it, a flustered "wah" mouth with two sweat drops. Nothing pinned under her arm or wing.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped_left but leaning toward the viewer's RIGHT, the tablet in the right sleeve-paw, the tail and scroll swinging to the other side, the tablet on her hood tilting the other way.
