# Claude

## 角色锁定

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — Claude, the anime-girl personification of Claude (Anthropic).
Personality: gentle, calm, bookish, a little shy, quietly cheerful; she thinks before she speaks.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy copper-orange hair, loose side-swept bangs, warm amber eyes.
- An orange daisy-like hair flower with a black striped ribbon bow and a white tassel on HER LEFT side (viewer's right); small drop earrings.
- Cream-white dress with a short cape collar, orange flower embroidery, orange and black front panels, small black bows with gold tassels, a black waist sash, an orange flower brooch at the collar; dark brown boots.
- Signature prop: a dark brown hardcover book with an orange flower emblem. Keep it in every image unless the pose says otherwise.
How she shows emotion: through the book (hugging it, hiding behind it, reading it) and small, soft expressions. Never wild or loud.
```

## 动作

每行「- 姿态: 动作」。没写的姿态用 art/姿态/ 里的默认动作；皮肤没写的用「基于」那个角色的。

- busy_left: Two jobs at once: body turned toward the viewer's LEFT, reading an open book held up on that side with one hand, while her other hand reaches back to steady a second open book that is starting to tip on the right side (still touching her fingers). Pressed lips, eyes darting, one small sweat drop, hair swinging slightly.
- busy_right: Same as busy-left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT: now reading the book on the right, the other hand steadying the tipping book on the left.
- edge_left: She is hiding beyond the LEFT border of the image and peeking in toward the right: the left border cuts through her body, her face and near hand are just inside it, the near hand gripping the left border. Clutching her book against her chest with the far hand while the near hand grips the border; a shy, gentle little smile, slightly blushing, as if quietly checking whether you need her.
- edge_left_peek: Still hugging her book to her chest, peeking over it with wide amber eyes, a shy curious little "oh?" and soft blush.
- edge_right: Same as edge_left but she is hiding beyond the RIGHT border and peeking in toward the left: the right border cuts through her body, the near hand gripping the right border. Same expression and props.
- edge_right_peek: Still hugging her book to her chest, peeking over it with wide amber eyes, a shy curious little "oh?" and soft blush.
- roll_call: Her book hugged against her chest with one arm, the other hand raised a little shyly, fingers together; a soft, slightly embarrassed smile with the mouth open in a gentle "Here."
- friend_up: Her book hugged up against her chest with both arms so its top edge hides her mouth and the lower half of her face; only her eyes show over it, softly curved in a happy smile, cheeks blushing a little.
- swamped_left: Too many jobs: hugging a small stack of three books against her chest with one arm, the top book sliding off toward the viewer's LEFT and caught by her fingertips, body leaning that way, one foot lifted, small swirl eyes, a wobbly little 'awawa' mouth, blushing, a few strands of hair loose.
- swamped_right: Same as swamped-left but the top book is sliding toward the viewer's RIGHT and she leans and catches it on that side.
