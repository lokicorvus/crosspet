# CrossPet 立绘：新角色 / 新皮肤制作说明（给用 API 生图的画师）

这份说明是给不碰 CrossPet 仓库、直接调用生图 API 的人用的。照着做，画出来的图交回给作者，由作者放进项目、跑机检、挑版本、导入。

文中 ` ```text ` 代码块里的英文就是要发给模型的提示词，复制时**一字不改**，只替换 `[方括号]` 里的占位内容。附录 A、B 是从项目里原样导出的规则段落和姿态表（导出日期 2026-11-18）。

---

## 0. API 怎么调

| 项                | 设置                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 接口               | **图片编辑接口**（edits），不要用纯文生图。每张图都要附参考图作为输入；附件按本文写的顺序传，第一张最重要                             |
| 透明桌宠图（绝大多数）      | `background: "transparent"`、`output_format: "png"`、`size: "1024x1024"`（能更大就更大，必须是正方形） |
| 选角色页展示图 portrait | `background: "opaque"`、`size: "1024x1536"`（2:3 竖图，带完整场景）                              |
| 质量               | 最高档；如果有 `input_fidelity` 这一项，开 `high`（让脸和服装贴近参考图）                                     |
| 张数               | `n = 1`，一次调用只出一张；每张只画 1 版                                                        |
| 上下文              | **每张图单独调用，不要在同一个对话或线程里连着画**。连着画时偏差会一张张累积（脸越画越写实、眼线越画越粗）。每张都只附本文指定的那几张图，不要附自己之前画过的其他姿态 |

提示词里写的 `transparent_background: true` 原本是给另一个画图工具看的，在 API 里只是一句文字，留着没关系，真正起作用的是 `background` 参数。

**开工前先确认**：GPT image 2.5 的 API 能不能输出真透明的 PNG（四角 alpha = 0）。不能的话先停下来告诉作者，因为透明是硬性要求，白底或者画出来的棋盘格都不行。

### 文件命名

`<角色id>__<姿态>__v<N>.png`，例如 `kimi__thinking__v1.png`。同一张每重画一次 N 就加 1，旧版不要删、不要覆盖。定妆图命名为 `<角色id>__idle__v<N>.png`。

### 交图前自己看一遍

- 背景是真透明（portrait 除外）；没有地板、投影、光晕、边框
- 头顶（呆毛、帽子、耳朵、角）完整地在画里，上方留一点空
- 普通姿态：人物约占画面高度的 90%，居中，脚在底边附近，身体不碰左右边框；和定妆 idle 并排放时，大小、脚的位置一致
- 没有字、对话框，也没有飘在空中的 ? ! 爱心 音符 Zzz 闪光（衣服上本来就有的图案不算）
- 单边的饰品（发夹、耳环、尾巴、异色瞳）在**她身体**的同一边
- 手指数量对，没有多出来的手，道具都拿在手里或贴着身体
- 脸和定妆图是同一个人：眼睛大小形状、眼线粗细都对得上，没有画得更写实、更成熟

## 1. 新角色流程

```
设定图/参考图 ──① 定妆 idle（画 1 版，作者看过再定）
                   │
                   ├─② idle-blink（以 idle 为底图，只改眼睛）
                   ├─③ 普通姿态（每张只附定妆 idle）
                   ├─④ 成对姿态：先画左，右边以画好的左边为底图改
                   ├─⑤ 贴边探头：在同侧的贴边图上改
                   ├─⑥ 时段 6 张 + 节日 8 张
                   └─⑦ 选角色页：portrait 展示图 + avatar 探头头像
```

每一步画完先交给作者验收，再画下一步。②～⑦ 都以 **作者选定的那张定妆 idle** 为参考，旧设定图从这一步起不再附。

### ① 定妆 idle

- 附件：1. 角色设定图 / 参考图（作者提供），这是唯一的设计和画风参考
- 只画 1 版交给作者看；作者不满意会说明哪里要改，再重画。

```text
Create one transparent-background full-body idle character sprite of [角色名] for the CrossPet desktop-pet project.

REFERENCE: The single attached image is the authoritative reference for BOTH the character design AND the drawing style. Redraw this same girl as a clean production sprite. Preserve her distinctive appearance and the way she is drawn; improve only the liveliness of her expression, with subtle natural adjustments. This is not a character redesign.

DRAWING STYLE: Match the reference's [比例与画法：例如 rounded chibi proportions about 2.5 heads tall, broad soft cheeks, small rounded chin, large round eyes, tiny hands and feet]. Keep the same clean delicately weighted outlines, soft two-tone anime cel shading and restrained soft highlights. Do not make the outlines heavier or add a sticker border. No realistic anatomy, glossy 3D rendering, painterly texture or heavy makeup. Eyes: [眼睛画法，例如 big round irises with a light gradient, legible dark pupils, two simple round highlights, a thin dark upper lash line without a winged tip].

EXPRESSION / IDLE POSE: She looks directly at the viewer with [表情：例如 a bright, attentive, delighted expression, as if she has just noticed a friend and is ready to listen]. BOTH EYES FULLY OPEN, looking straight at the viewer (this image is the base for the blink frame). [眉毛、嘴、头的倾斜]. Body upright facing forward, feet together, [手的位置]. No large gesture. Convey emotion through gaze, eyebrows, cheeks and mouth, not floating effects.

DESIGN LOCK:
- [发型、发色]
- [眼睛颜色；异色瞳写明 HER RIGHT eye (viewer's left) / HER LEFT eye (viewer's right)]
- [头饰、单边饰品：写明在 HER LEFT / HER RIGHT 哪一边，并写 Do not mirror these ornaments]
- [衣服从上到下逐件写]
- [鞋袜]
- [耳朵、尾巴、翅膀等身体特征]
- Add no text, letters or symbols beyond those that are part of the costume.

OUTPUT: Exactly one character in one square image, at least 1024 by 1024. Genuine transparent alpha background (transparent_background: true), not white and not a painted checkerboard. Full body centered, about 90% of the canvas height, with clear transparent margins above the head, beside all hair and accessories, and below both shoes. Every accessory and hair tip fully inside the frame. Clean bounded edges that work on both light and dark desktops. No floor, cast shadow, glow, halo, frame, text caption, speech bubble, floating notes, hearts or sparkles.
```

定妆选定后，作者会把下面这段**角色锁定**定下来（可以请画师照定妆图起草），后面每张图都要用：

```text
CHARACTER LOCK — [角色名], the anime-girl personification of [对应 AI], [一句话身份].
Personality: [性格，3～5 个词]. [她平时的默认表情].
Keep exactly (match the attached reference):
- [发型发色]
- [眼睛]
- [饰品及所在边：HER LEFT side (viewer's right)…]
- [衣服逐件]
- [鞋袜]
- [身体特征：耳朵 / 尾巴 / 角]
- HEIGHT LOCK: [最高点，例如 the tip of her ahoge] is always the highest point of the image, at the same height as in the reference. Nothing (hands, props) may go higher than it.
Palette: [主色]. No other strong colors.
How she shows emotion: [她特有的肢体表达：尾巴、耳朵、呆毛、道具……]
```

### ②～⑦ 每个姿态的提示词怎么拼

每张图的提示词按下面的顺序，**用一个空行隔开**拼成一整段：

1. **规则段**：附录 B 里这个姿态「规则」后面列的那几段，按列出的顺序，原文取自附录 A。段落里的 `{SIDE}` 换成 `LEFT` 或 `RIGHT`（附录 B 的「方向」）。
2. **角色锁定**：上面那段 CHARACTER LOCK。
3. **成对段**（只有成对的姿态才有）：
   - 附录 B 里写了「成对」（例如 busy_left 的成对是 busy_right），说明这张是先画的，加附录 A 的「成对-先画」：`{SIDE}` 换成本张的方向，`{PARTNER}` 换成 `<成对姿态>.png`，例如 `busy_right.png`。
   - 附录 B 里写了「底图」和「方向」，而且规则里**没有**「探头」，说明这张是后画的，加「成对-后画」：`{SIDE}` 换成本张方向，`{OTHER}` 换成另一边，`{BASE}` 换成 `<底图姿态>.png`。
4. **姿态行**，两行：
   ```text
   POSE (<姿态>): <动作>
   Save as: <角色id>__<姿态>__vN.png
   ```
   「动作」用作者给这个角色写的专属动作；作者没写的，用附录 B 里的「默认动作」。

**附件**：

- 有「底图」的姿态（idle-blink、各个 \_right、各个 \_peek），第 1 张附底图：作者选定的那一版；没选过就用版本号最大的。
- 然后附定妆 idle。
- 没有底图的姿态只附定妆 idle 一张。

**画的顺序**：底图必须先画好并经作者选定。顺序是 idle-blink → 普通姿态 → busy_left → busy_right → swamped_left → swamped_right → edge_left → edge_right → edge_left_peek → edge_right_peek → 时段节日 → portrait → avatar。

**眨眼帧**：只改眼睛，其他部分要和 idle 完全一样。程序只会取眼睛那一块贴回 idle，所以眼睛以外的地方有一点差异没关系，但构图不能变。

**portrait / avatar**：规则段分别只用「展示图」「头像」，**不要**加「通用」。portrait 用 `background: "opaque"`、1024×1536；avatar 是透明方图。

**一套新角色要画的姿态**（附录 B 里除了以下几类，其余全部要画）：

- 标着 DeepSeek 专属、Gemini 专属的姿态不画（deepthink、eat、feast、pout、slack、swim、float）
- friend_up（关系升级）这次不画
- 彩蛋姿态另外出题，不在这份说明里

#### 拼好的样子（示例：Kimi 的 busy_right）

附件：1. `kimi__busy_left__v4.png`（底图）　2. Kimi 定妆 idle

```text
[附录 A「通用」全文]

[附录 A「多会话」全文]

[Kimi 的 CHARACTER LOCK]

TWO-FRAME PAIR (very important): this image is the RIGHT partner of "busy_left.png", which is already finished and attached as the FIRST attachment.
- EDIT that finished image: … （「成对-后画」全文，SIDE=RIGHT，OTHER=LEFT，BASE=busy_left.png）

POSE (busy_right): Edit busy_left into the corresponding viewer's RIGHT working frame. …
Save as: kimi__busy_right__vN.png
```

## 2. 新皮肤流程

皮肤就是已有角色换一身衣服。有两条路，**现在都走 A**（魔术师 Gemini、毛衣 DeepSeek 都是这样做的）。

### A. 整套皮肤（推荐）

和新角色一样：先画皮肤的定妆 idle，作者选定后，用它画整套。区别有两处：

1. **定妆附两张图，分工不同**：
   - 附件 1：原角色现在用的 idle（作者提供），只用来参考画法、身材、比例、大小和位置，衣服不看
   - 附件 2：皮肤的衣服设计图或草稿（作者提供），只用来参考衣服，不照它的画法
2. **动作沿用原角色**：皮肤没单独写的姿态，用原角色的专属动作（作者提供原角色的动作表）。动作里提到原角色衣服或道具的地方，如果和新衣服冲突，就换成皮肤自己的。

皮肤定妆提示词：

```text
Draw a desktop-pet sprite of an existing chibi character in a new outfit. Two images are attached, and they have DIFFERENT jobs:
- Attached image 1 is the original [角色名] sprite. It is the reference for HOW SHE IS DRAWN: line weight, line color, shading, the way her eyes are drawn, her head and body proportions, her scale and her position in the image. Ignore its outfit.
- Attached image 2 is [a rough draft / a design sheet] of [角色名] in her "[皮肤名]" outfit. Use it ONLY for the outfit: [逐项列出：帽子、外套、上衣、裙子、鞋、道具] and their colors. Do NOT copy the way it is drawn.

STYLE: draw her the way image 1 is drawn: [线条：例如 thin, soft, even lineart in dark violet (not pure black)]; the outer outline is the same thin line, not a thick sticker border; soft two-tone cel shading with the same hair highlights as image 1. Her eyes are big and round like in image 1, with simple round highlights, clearly dark pupils and a dark upper lash line; no heavy eyeliner, no long winged lashes, no narrowed or slanted eye shape. Keep the outfit colors as rich as in image 2; do not fade or grey them out.

FORMAT:
- Real transparent background (generate with transparent_background: true). No floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no frame.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols floating in the air.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, body facing the viewer. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as image 1.
- A continuous, closed thin dark outline around the whole silhouette.
- Chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes, the same proportions as image 1.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger.

CHARACTER LOCK — [角色名] as the "[皮肤名]": an alternate skin of [角色名], [原角色一句话身份]. Same girl, same personality: [性格].
Keep exactly (from image 1):
- [发型发色、呆毛]
- [眼睛；异色瞳写明哪边]
- [耳朵 / 尾巴 / 角等身体特征]
- [保留的原饰品，写明在 HER LEFT / HER RIGHT]
Outfit (from image 2):
- [逐件写，写清楚尺寸和位置，例如帽子不能高过呆毛、不能盖住耳朵；披风不挡住身体前面]
- SCALE LOCK: her body is the same size and her feet are in the same place as in image 1. This image sets the scale for the whole skin.
Palette: [主色]. No other strong colors.
How she shows emotion: [和原角色一致的肢体表达，加上新衣服的道具]

POSE (idle, the base image for the whole skin): standing calmly with her body facing the viewer. [手的位置、道具]. Head upright. BOTH EYES FULLY OPEN, big and round, looking straight at the viewer (this image is the base for the blink frame). [眉毛、嘴、腮红]. [耳朵、尾巴的状态].
```

定妆选定后，皮肤的 CHARACTER LOCK 就用上面那段（去掉 image 1、image 2 的说法，改成 "match the attached reference"），再按第 1 节 ②～⑦ 的拼法画整套，附件只附**皮肤的定妆 idle**。

**定妆常见问题**（魔术师 Gemini 的教训）：

- 提示词写了「一只眼微眯」，结果两只眼都变窄变利。定妆图要求两只眼都完全睁开、又大又圆。
- 外轮廓比原版粗，像贴纸。要明确写 thin outline、not a sticker border。
- 帽子盖住耳朵或者把人压矮。要写清楚帽子的大小、位置和高度上限。
- 画风段只写怎么画（线条、阴影、眼睛的画法），不要写「照抄脸」；表情要写成画得出来的东西（眼睛多大、看哪里、眉毛、嘴），不要只写心情。

### B. 换衣皮肤（只换衣服，备用）

用原角色每个姿态现成的成品图当底图，只改衣服，脸和动作完全保留。适合「新模型从头画会把脸画走样」的情况。缺点是作者要先导出原角色每个姿态的底图（放大到 1254×1254 的透明方图），而且原角色没有的姿态还是得走 A。

- 附件：1. 原角色这个姿态的底图（作者导出）　2. 皮肤衣服参考图
- 提示词：附录 A「换衣」全文，替换三处占位：
  - `{POSE}` 换成姿态名
  - `{OUTFIT}` 换成衣服描述（逐件写）
  - `{EDGE}`：贴边和探头姿态换成下面这句（SIDE 是 LEFT 或 RIGHT），其他姿态直接删掉  
    `\n- She is cut off by the {SIDE} border of the image exactly as in the first image: keep the cut in the same place, with no outline along it.`
- 最后另起一行，空一行后写 `Save as: <皮肤id>__<姿态>__vN.png`
- 眨眼帧不走换衣：以皮肤自己的 idle 为底图，按第 1 节的方法画。

## 附录 A：规则段落原文（art/规则/*.txt）

### 通用

```text
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached images are the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Real transparent background (generate with transparent_background: true). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep complete, clean edges. If an attached reference image has a plain white background, that is only because it was made before transparent output was available: do NOT copy the white background.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, horns, ears, wings and tail included), so the edges stay clean against any background.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact path given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.
```

### 原地

```text
STANDING IN PLACE: "running" here means she is running a command on the computer, NOT running on foot. She stands still with both feet planted on the ground, body upright and facing the viewer like the reference. No running, walking, striding, hopping or kicking: no lifted leg, no mid-step, no leaning forward into motion, no hair or cloak flying back from speed. Show "hard at work" only with her face, arms, hands and small props.
```

### 多会话

```text
MULTI-TASKING ANIMATION FRAMES: she is handling several jobs at once. The app flips quickly between the LEFT frame and the RIGHT frame, so she looks like she is turning back and forth, busy on one side, then the other. Each image must read as "mid-action, a bit hectic", never as a static pose.

POSE EXCEPTION to the format rules (for these images only): she does NOT have to stand straight and face the viewer. She may lean, twist her body toward one side, turn her head, lift one foot slightly off balance, and her hair, ribbons, wings and tail may swing with the motion. Keep only these layout rules: the whole character is inside the image, same scale as the reference (about 90% of the image height), feet near the bottom, roughly centered.

KEEP IT CUTE AND GRACEFUL (very important):
- NO T-pose, NO arms spread straight out wide, NO raised-arms "surrender" pose, NO armpits showing. Elbows stay naturally bent; hands work close to her body, at most reaching a little past her shoulders.
- Asymmetric and natural: one hand actively working, the other reaching or steadying something; head turned toward the side she is working on.
- Expressions stay small and pretty: a flustered pout, pressed lips, a small "wah" mouth, a sweat drop on the cheek. NO wide-open screaming mouth, no ugly grimace.
- Every prop must TOUCH her or her hands (things can be tipping or slipping, but still touching). Nothing floats detached in the air: the app deletes detached pieces. A few short motion lines drawn right against her arm or hair are fine.
```

### 成对-先画

```text
TWO-FRAME PAIR: this image is the first of a left/right pair ("{PARTNER}" will be drawn later as an edit of this one). Make the pose clearly directional toward the viewer's {SIDE}, so the partner can turn the other way. "left" and "right" mean the viewer's left and right.
```

### 成对-后画

```text
TWO-FRAME PAIR (very important): this image is the {SIDE} partner of "{BASE}", which is already finished and attached as the FIRST attachment.
- EDIT that finished image: same character, same outfit, same props, same scale, same image size, feet at the same height. Only the direction changes: now everything that faced or leaned toward the viewer's {OTHER} faces or leans toward the viewer's {SIDE}, and the working hand / steadying hand swap roles.
- Do NOT mirror-flip the image. One-sided details stay on the SAME side of HER body as in the CHARACTER LOCK and the reference (hair ornaments, braids, earrings, horn shapes, the side the tail comes out on, eye colors). After turning, those details may move to the other side of the picture: that is correct.
- "left" and "right" always mean the viewer's left and right.
```

### 贴边

```text
EDGE-PEEK FRAMES: the desktop pet can be parked at the left or right edge of the user's screen. She hides behind the screen edge and peeks in. The app lines up the BORDER of the image with the edge of the screen, so the image border itself is the screen edge she is holding on to.

POSE EXCEPTION to the format rules (for these images only): she is NOT fully visible and NOT centered. The image border cuts straight down through her body; only about 55-65% of her (her face, both eyes, the near shoulder and arm, part of her body and skirt) is inside the picture, the rest is hidden beyond the border. Keep these rules: same scale as the reference (if she were fully visible she would be about 90% of the image height: same head size as the reference), head near the top but with a clear band of empty transparent background above her hair (the whole top of her head, including ears, horns, hats and ahoge, must be inside the picture), the visible part of her feet / skirt hem near the bottom. The cut along the border is a clean straight cut, no outline along it. Everything else in the format rules still applies (transparent background elsewhere, closed outline on all other sides, no floating symbols).

KEEP IT CUTE: her near hand grips the very border of the image as if holding the edge of a door frame; her head tilts slightly into the picture; hair, ribbons and tail drape naturally around the edge. NOT falling, NOT climbing; calm and a little playful, like peeking around a corner.
```

### 探头

```text
EDIT the attached base image (the first attachment). It is the exact model reference: keep her head size, body proportions, outfit, hair, accessories, colors and art style EXACTLY the same, and keep the image border on the {SIDE} cutting straight down through her body (a clean straight cut, no outline along it), same image size, same scale, feet at the same height, the whole top of her head still inside the picture.
Change ONLY this: the user's mouse cursor just hovered over her, so she leans her head and upper body out from behind the {SIDE} border to look at the viewer with curiosity. Now about 70% of her is visible.
HOW SHE LEANS OUT (very important):
