# 千问 · 手忙脚乱与贴边（6 张 + 探出来 2 张）

附件：`docs/prompts/ref-qwen.webp`

## 手忙脚乱（4 张）

她同时有好几个对话在干活时，程序在左右两张之间快速切换。千金的手忙脚乱：一边打电话吩咐、一边看平板。

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Real transparent background (generate with transparent_background: true). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep complete, clean edges. If an attached reference image has a plain white background, that is only because it was made before transparent output was available: do NOT copy the white background.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, hat, ribbons, tassels, sleeves and skirt included), so the edges stay clean against any background.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, long flowing hair, fine ornament details.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, hat, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.
- Every prop she holds TOUCHES her hand; nothing floats in the air. Props are small and simple so they still read at about 200 px.

CHARACTER LOCK — Qwen, the anime-girl personification of Qwen (Alibaba's AI), a gentle and generous young lady from a wealthy family.
Personality: warm, gracious, generous and well-mannered; she loves treating everyone (buying milk tea for the whole office is normal to her) and thinks sharing good things is simply natural. Never arrogant, never mean: a soft, kind "rich big-sister" smile. A little sheltered and easygoing about money, which makes her endearing rather than snobbish.
Keep exactly:
- Very long, wavy periwinkle-blue hair down past the waist, side-swept bangs, a braided crown on her left side (viewer's right) and one long braid falling over her left shoulder (viewer's right).
- Big round violet-blue eyes with bright highlights, soft pink blush.
- A small dark-navy hat worn tilted on the viewer's left side of her head, with a white fur trim, a white ribbon bow, a gold-framed round blue emblem (a white stylized knot shape) and small blue tassels.
- Gold drop earrings with small blue gems.
- White mandarin-collar dress with gold frog buttons and faint white floral embroidery; a dark-navy wide-sleeved over-robe with gold Greek-key borders; a blue front skirt panel with a blue ribbon bow and a round emblem brooch at the waist; blue tassels with gold knots hanging from the waist; a white frilly underskirt; white gloves; navy heels with small gold anklet charms.
- Signature prop: a blue folding fan with gold ribs and a white leaf pattern. Keep it in her hand in most poses unless the pose says otherwise.
- Secondary prop: a small white-and-gold handbag with the same round emblem. Draw it ONLY when a pose mentions it.
Palette: periwinkle blue, dark navy, white and soft gold. Keep it elegant; no other strong colors.
How she shows emotion: gracefully, mostly with the fan. Laughing = fan raised to cover her mouth with smiling closed eyes; thinking = closed fan tapping her chin; shy = fan hiding the lower half of her face; pleased = fan opened with a gentle flourish; upset = fan snapped shut. Expressions are bright and open (like the reference's happy open-mouth smile), but always gentle and lady-like, never loud.

MULTI-TASKING ANIMATION FRAMES: she is handling several jobs at once. The app flips quickly between the LEFT frame and the RIGHT frame, so she looks like she is turning back and forth, busy on one side, then the other. Each image must read as "mid-action, a bit hectic", never as a static pose.

POSE EXCEPTION to the format rules (for these images only): she does NOT have to stand straight and face the viewer. She may lean, twist her body toward one side, turn her head, lift one foot slightly off balance, and her hair, braid, ribbons and tassels may swing with the motion. Keep only these layout rules: the whole character is inside the image, same scale as the reference (about 90% of the image height), feet near the bottom, roughly centered.

KEEP IT CUTE AND GRACEFUL (very important):
- NO T-pose, NO arms spread straight out wide, NO raised-arms "surrender" pose. Elbows stay naturally bent; hands work close to her body, at most reaching a little past her shoulders.
- Asymmetric and natural: one hand actively working, the other reaching or steadying something; head turned toward the side she is working on.
- Expressions stay small and pretty: a flustered pout, pressed lips, a small "wah" mouth, a sweat drop on the cheek. NO wide-open screaming mouth.
- Every prop must TOUCH her or her hands. Nothing floats detached in the air.

TWO-FRAME CONSISTENCY:
- Draw the "_left" image first. Then draw the "_right" image as an EDIT of the "_left" image you just made: same character, same outfit, same props, same scale and position. Only the direction changes.
- Do NOT simply mirror-flip the image: her hat (worn on the viewer's left) and her braid (over the viewer's right shoulder) must stay on the same side of her body as in the reference.
- "left" and "right" mean the viewer's left and right.

POSES (one image each, exact filename, draw them in this order):
busy_left.png    — graceful multitasking: turned toward the viewer's LEFT, tapping a slim white tablet held on that side, a white smartphone tucked between her cheek and shoulder, the closed fan tucked into her sash; calm focused face, one small sweat drop at her temple, braid swaying.
busy_right.png   — same as busy_left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT, the tablet now on the right side.
swamped_left.png — her composure slipping: leaning toward the viewer's LEFT tapping the tablet, the phone at her ear, and her other hand holding up the open handbag that is stuffed with papers, a forced gentle smile with two sweat drops, slightly wide eyes, hat a little askew.
swamped_right.png — same as swamped_left but leaning toward the viewer's RIGHT, the tablet, phone and handbag swapping sides accordingly.
```

## 探出来（2 张，用上面画好的贴边图当底图改）

导入时用 `SKIP_EDGE=left` / `right`。每张单独发：附件换成刚画好的 `edge_left.png` / `edge_right.png`。生图时打开透明背景（`transparent_background: true`）。

2026-10-20 重画：第一版要求「露出 80%」又要「近处的手还抓着边框」，模型只能把胳膊拉成一长条（loki：手太长、诡异）。这版改成身子还贴着边框、只靠上半身和头往外探，手就在肩膀旁边扶着边框。

```
EDIT the attached image. It is the exact model reference: keep her head size, body proportions, outfit, hair, accessories, colors and art style EXACTLY the same, and keep the image border on the LEFT cutting straight down through her body (a clean straight cut, no outline along it), same image size, same scale, feet at the same height.
Change ONLY this: the user's mouse cursor just hovered over her, so she leans her head and upper body out from behind the LEFT border to look at the viewer with curiosity. Now about 70% of her is visible.
HOW SHE LEANS OUT (very important):
- Her near shoulder and her hips stay close to the LEFT border; she does NOT step away from it. The lean comes from tilting her upper body and head into the picture.
- Her near hand rests on the border right next to her near shoulder, elbow clearly bent, the fingers curling around the edge as if holding a door frame. The distance from her shoulder to that hand is less than half her head height.
- Chibi arm proportions exactly as in the reference: short arms. NO outstretched arm, NO long stretched arm or sleeve reaching across the picture, NO arm pointing straight at the border.
- Her far hand holds the open fan lowered in front of her chest.
Expression: head tilted with curiosity, eyes open wide and looking straight at the viewer, a delighted "oh, it's you~" smile and a light blush. Hair, braid and tassels sway slightly from leaning out.
Transparent background everywhere else (no white background, no floor, no shadow), closed dark outline on all other sides, no text, no question marks or any floating symbols.
Save as: edge_left_peek.png
```
