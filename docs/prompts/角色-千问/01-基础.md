# 千问 · 基础动作（12 张）

附件：`docs/prompts/ref-qwen.webp`（设定稿，只附这一张）

最常出现的动作，先画这一批。画完缩到约 200px 看：帽子、扇子、长卷发和辫子都认得出；表情是温柔大方的千金，不是傲娇。

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Real transparent background (generate with transparent_background: true). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep complete, clean edges. If an attached reference image has a plain white background, that is only because it was made before transparent output was available: do NOT copy the white background.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, hat, ribbons, tassels, sleeves and skirt included), so the edges stay clean against any background.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, long flowing hair, fine ornament details.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, hat, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.
- Every prop she holds TOUCHES her hand; nothing floats in the air. Props are small and simple so they still read at about 200 px.

CHARACTER LOCK — Qwen, the anime-girl personification of Qwen (Alibaba's AI), a gentle and generous young lady from a wealthy family.
Personality: warm, gracious, generous and well-mannered; she loves treating everyone (buying milk tea for the whole office is normal to her) and thinks sharing good things is simply natural. Never arrogant, never mean: a soft, kind "rich big-sister" smile. A little sheltered and easygoing about money, which makes her endearing rather than snobbish.
Keep exactly:
- Very long, wavy periwinkle-blue hair down past the waist, side-swept bangs, a braided crown on her left side (viewer's right) and one long braid falling over her left shoulder (viewer's right).
- Big round violet-blue eyes with bright highlights, soft pink blush.
- A small dark-navy hat worn tilted on the viewer's left side of her head, with a white fur trim, a white ribbon bow, a gold-framed round blue emblem (a white stylized knot shape) and small blue tassels.
- Gold drop earrings with small blue gems.
- White mandarin-collar dress with gold frog buttons and faint white floral embroidery; a dark-navy wide-sleeved over-robe with gold Greek-key borders; a blue front skirt panel with a blue ribbon bow and a round emblem brooch at the waist; blue tassels with gold knots hanging from the waist; a white frilly underskirt; white gloves; navy heels with small gold anklet charms.
- Signature prop: a blue folding fan with gold ribs and a white leaf pattern. Keep it in her hand in most poses unless the pose says otherwise.
- Secondary prop: a small white-and-gold handbag with the same round emblem. Draw it ONLY when a pose mentions it.
Palette: periwinkle blue, dark navy, white and soft gold. Keep it elegant; no other strong colors.
How she shows emotion: gracefully, mostly with the fan. Laughing = fan raised to cover her mouth with smiling closed eyes; thinking = closed fan tapping her chin; shy = fan hiding the lower half of her face; pleased = fan opened with a gentle flourish; upset = fan snapped shut. Expressions are bright and open (like the reference's happy open-mouth smile), but always gentle and lady-like, never loud.

POSES (one image each, exact filename, in this order):
idle-blink.png — an EDIT of the attached reference: change ONLY the eyes to softly closed in a gentle smile. Everything else must stay pixel-identical (it is swapped in for a blink animation).
listening.png  — attentive and kind: leaning slightly forward, eyes a little wider, a soft "hm?" smile, the open fan held low at her side, the other hand raised a little toward the viewer as if saying "go on".
thinking.png   — the closed fan tapping her chin, eyes looking up and to the side, a calm pondering smile.
reading.png    — reading a small hardcover book with a navy cover and gold corners, held open in both gloved hands, the closed fan tucked into her waist sash, intent and pleased.
writing.png    — writing with a slim gold fountain pen in a small navy leather notebook held against her other forearm, focused, a tiny smile; the closed fan tucked into her waist sash.
running.png    — giving instructions like a young lady of the house: holding a slim white smartphone to her ear with one hand, the other hand pointing forward with the closed fan, a confident kind smile ("please take care of this for me~").
searching.png  — holding a small gold lorgnette (opera glasses on a handle) up to her eyes with one hand, curious, the closed fan in the other hand.
happy.png      — very happy: the open fan raised beside her face, eyes closed in a bright open-mouth smile, light blush, a small graceful hop with tassels and braid swaying.
oops.png       — flustered: the open fan held up covering her mouth, wide worried eyes, one sweat drop on her cheek, shoulders raised a little.
surprised.png  — startled: eyes wide open, mouth a small "o", leaning back a little, the fan half-raised in surprise.
pat.png        — being patted on the head: eyes closed, blissful, blushing, hiding the lower half of her face shyly behind the open fan, hat slightly pushed askew.
sleeping.png   — dozing while standing: eyes closed, head drooped to one side, the closed fan held loosely against her chest with both hands, peaceful.
```
