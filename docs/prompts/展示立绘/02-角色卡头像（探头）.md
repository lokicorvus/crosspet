## 怎么用

1. 生图工具开 `transparent_background: true`，尺寸选**方形 1024×1024**。
2. 每张单独发，按顺序拼：下面的「头像格式规则」→ 这个角色的「角色锁定」（整段复制 `CHARACTER LOCK`）→ 这张的「POSE」那一行。
3. **构图故意留余量**：头画小一点，上面和右边多留空白。Claude 导入时放进设置页看效果，按卡片比例裁，顺便把 7 张头的大小、位置调齐；只有「从下边探出来、手扒着下边」这个姿势本身不对才需要重画。
4. **先画 DeepSeek 一张试稿**，满意了再画其余 6 张；其余的都把 DeepSeek 试稿也附上，说明「style reference only」。
5. 画好放进上表的 `raw/` 文件夹，告诉 Claude 导入（裁好后给你看截图）。

## 卡片上会遮住什么

小卡大约 80×92（宽×高），**下面约 1/5 会被一条半透明的名字栏盖住**，右上角有时会有一个「4 套」的小标签。所以脸（尤其眼睛）要在画面中间偏上，扒着下边的小手被名字栏挡一点没关系。

## 头像格式规则（每张都贴在最前面）

```
You are drawing a small CHARACTER-SELECT AVATAR for an existing chibi character: she peeks up from the BOTTOM-LEFT corner of the picture, like peeking over the edge of a desk. The first attached image is the ONLY design reference for the character. The second attached image (the CrossPet app icon) is a FRAMING reference only: copy how the blue-haired maid girl in its BOTTOM-LEFT corner peeks up (head and two little hands over the bottom edge), but ignore everything else in it.

FORMAT RULES:
- Transparent background: real transparency (alpha). No scenery, no desk, no floor, no shadow, no glow, no frame, no card border. The app draws the card background itself. If an attached reference image has a plain white background, that is only because it was made before transparent output was available: do NOT copy the white background.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos other than those already on her outfit, no speech bubbles, no floating symbols or effects.
- Square image, 1024x1024. Exactly one character.
- Framing: only her head, a bit of her shoulders and her two small hands are visible. Her body is hidden below the BOTTOM edge of the image; the bottom edge cuts straight across her shoulders (a clean straight cut, no outline along it). Her two hands rest on / grip the bottom edge, as if pulling herself up to peek.
- She is placed toward the LEFT: her head is in the lower-left two thirds of the image, and the left edge may cut a little of her hair (clean straight cut). The upper-right area stays mostly empty (transparent), apart from hair, ears, horns or hat that naturally reach there.
- LEAVE GENEROUS ROOM (the picture will be cropped later): her head is about 50% of the image height, with plenty of empty transparent space above her head and to the right. Her eyes sit a little above the middle of her visible part, because the bottom fifth of the final card will be covered by a name label. The only edge that cuts her is the BOTTOM edge (and optionally a little of her hair at the left edge).
- Head slightly tilted, looking straight at the viewer. Keep the whole head inside the picture except where the left edge cuts a little hair; the top of her head (and ears / horns / hat) must not be cut.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the silhouette, except along the straight cuts at the image edges.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi proportions, big head, round soft shapes, big expressive eyes. Hair, accessories, outfit (the visible collar and sleeves), colors and features stay EXACTLY as in the reference.
```
