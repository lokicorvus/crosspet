# 冬装 DeepSeek · 定妆（2 张）

附件（按顺序）：`docs/prompts/ref-deepseek.webp`（身材比例、画风、站姿照它）+ `characters/deepseek-winter/raw/ref/design.png`（你的冬装三视图，衣服照它）

三视图的画风比我们的更细、表情更淡；这一步要把衣服「穿」到我们的 DeepSeek 身上，**画风、比例、表情以第一张为准**。

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. Two images are attached: the FIRST is the exact body, scale, pose and art-style reference (DeepSeek standing idle); the SECOND is a three-view outfit design sheet (front / side / back) to put on her. Use the SECOND image only for the clothes; the art style, proportions, face and line quality must follow the FIRST image.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Real transparent background (generate with transparent_background: true). Nothing else: no floor, no ground shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep complete, clean edges. If an attached reference image has a plain white background, that is only because it was made before transparent output was available: do NOT copy the white background.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, ears, hat or hood, tail included).
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.

CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek in her winter whale coat: an alternate skin of DeepSeek, the blue whale girl (anime-girl personification of DeepSeek), bundled up warm for the cold.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat. Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly (body from DeepSeek, outfit from the winter design):
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips, flowing out from under the hood. Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush.
- A navy-blue whale-shaped hood worn up: a cute whale face on top of the hood (two small dark eyes, pink blush cheeks), a white water-spout puff on the crown, small fin flaps on both sides (they take the place of her whale-fin ears), a thick white fluffy trim around her face, a tiny white whale pin on the side, and white pom-poms hanging from navy ribbon ties on both sides.
- A striped pale-blue and white knit scarf with fringed ends, wrapped around her neck.
- A navy winter coat-dress: white fluffy trim at the cuffs and hem, a navy bow and white pom-pom buttons down the front, small white whale prints and snowflakes, a white wave pattern along the hem; round white fluffy mittens with a small navy bow (her hands look like soft white paws).
- Navy winter boots with white fur tops.
- Her big whale tail, dark navy fading to blue, curling out from behind her on the viewer's right.
- HOOD LOCK: the whale hood is ALWAYS worn up, in every image, never pushed back or taken off, and its white water-spout puff is always fully visible on top. The top of the water spout is the highest point of the image, at the same height as in the reference; nothing (hands, props) may go higher than it, so every sprite keeps the same scale.
Palette: deep navy and white with pale-blue accents and tiny snowflakes. Warm and cozy winter.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the hood's water-spout puff bounces; the pom-poms swing; the scarf ends flutter.

POSES (one image each, exact filename, in this order):
idle.png       — EDIT of the FIRST attached image: same pose, same scale, same position, same face and expression (a cheerful open smile), same hair and tail; only change her clothes and head accessories to the winter outfit described above (remove the maid headdress and maid dress; the whale hood replaces the headdress). Front view only, facing the viewer.
idle-blink.png — an EDIT of the idle.png you just drew: change ONLY the eyes to softly closed. Everything else must stay pixel-identical (it is swapped in for a blink animation).
```
