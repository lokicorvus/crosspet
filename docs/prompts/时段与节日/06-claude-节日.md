# Claude · 节日登场（8 张）

附件：`ref-claude.webp`

按日期顺序，万圣节最急（10-31）。

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, horns, ears, wings and tail included), so no white part of her ever touches the white background directly.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.

CHARACTER LOCK — Claude, the anime-girl personification of Claude (Anthropic).
Personality: gentle, calm, bookish, a little shy, quietly cheerful; she thinks before she speaks.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy copper-orange hair, loose side-swept bangs, warm amber eyes.
- An orange daisy-like hair flower with a black striped ribbon bow and a white tassel on HER LEFT side (viewer's right); small drop earrings.
- Cream-white dress with a short cape collar, orange flower embroidery, orange and black front panels, small black bows with gold tassels, a black waist sash, an orange flower brooch at the collar; dark brown boots.
- Signature prop: a dark brown hardcover book with an orange flower emblem. Keep it in every image unless the pose says otherwise.
How she shows emotion: through the book (hugging it, hiding behind it, reading it) and small, soft expressions. Never wild or loud.

STYLE NOTE (Claude): keep every pose soft and modest; the book appears in almost every pose (hugged, held, read, hidden behind).

FESTIVAL GREETINGS: each image below is a one-time greeting she plays on the first appearance of a holiday. Same framing, scale, outline and background rules as every other sprite.
FESTIVAL EXCEPTION to the format rules: for these images ONLY, she may wear festive accessories or a festive outer layer (a hat, cape, scarf, jacket) and hold a festive prop. Her face, hair color and style, ears / horns / wings / tail, and her signature hair ornament must stay exactly as in the reference so she is instantly recognizable. Still: NO text, NO letters or Chinese characters (no 福, no greetings, no numbers or years), no flags, no logos; background stays pure white with no scenery.

POSES (one image each, exact filename):
fest_halloween.png — Halloween (Oct 31): A small black witch hat with an orange flower on the band, holding a small carved jack-o'-lantern lantern in one hand and her book in the other, a gentle shy smile.
fest_christmas.png — Christmas (Dec 25): A red Santa hat with white fur trim, a soft red scarf, holding a small wrapped gift shaped like a book with an orange ribbon, warm smile.
fest_newyear.png — New Year's Day (Jan 1): A small striped party hat, holding a party popper with a little burst of paper streamers coming out, eyes closed in a bright smile.
fest_spring.png — Spring Festival / Chinese New Year: A short red Chinese-style padded jacket with gold frog buttons over her dress, holding a round red paper lantern with a gold tassel, gentle festive smile.
fest_lantern.png — Lantern Festival: Holding a white rabbit-shaped paper lantern glowing softly, gentle smile.
fest_dragonboat.png — Dragon Boat Festival: Holding a small zongzi (leaf-wrapped rice dumpling tied with string) in one hand and her book in the other, gentle smile.
fest_midautumn.png — Mid-Autumn Festival: Holding a round mooncake on a small plate, a rabbit lantern hanging from her wrist, soft smile.
fest_national.png — National Day Golden Week (Oct 1): holiday travel theme, NOT political, no flags: A wide-brimmed sun hat, pulling a small rolling suitcase, book under her arm, cheerful travel smile.
```
