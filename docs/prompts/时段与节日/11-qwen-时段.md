# Qwen · 时段登场（6 张）

附件：`characters/qwen/idle.webp`

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF), or a genuinely transparent background when using the transparent-PNG import path. Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette, so no white part of her ever touches the white background directly.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.

CHARACTER LOCK — Qwen, a Chinese-inspired chibi girl, anime-girl personification of Qwen (Alibaba / 通义千问, literally "a thousand questions").
Personality: endlessly curious and warmly chatty. She asks thoughtful questions before she acts and is a patient explainer who never makes you feel stupid. She enjoys late-night reading and gently fans herself while thinking.
Keep exactly:
- Soft light BLUE hair with a thick side braid on the viewer's right, tied with a small white bow. Match the blue hair in the reference; never recolor it purple. Keep the hair compact around the shoulders and skirt. NO animal ears, tail or question-mark ahoge.
- A small tilted dark indigo four-corner scholar cap with pale trim and a white side bow. Keep the same cap silhouette in every pose.
- Big round violet eyes, gentle warm expression. No forehead gem.
- Chinese-inspired pale high-collar blouse with neat blue knot closures, short layered blue-and-cream skirt, broad dark navy outer sleeves with simple pale diamond trim, sash with a small crescent cloth pouch tied close to the body, white socks and blue shoes. Keep the body compact and round: approximately 2.5 heads tall and a broad silhouette like the reference, never a slim adult body.
- Signature prop: a small blue folding fan with pale cloud bands and a crescent motif. Keep it physically gripped by one hand in most poses, open or closed as needed. The crescent pouch remains tied at the sash; accessories never float.
Her blue scholar cap, braid, fan and layered clothing distinguish her from Gemini. Never add Gemini's cat ears, star ornaments, galaxy motifs or tail. Keep clean dark line art and broad flat colors with two-step shading.
How she shows emotion: the head tilts, braid and sleeves sway, and the held folding fan opens, closes or pauses. Her blinks are slow and deliberate.

TIME-OF-DAY GREETINGS: each image below is a one-time greeting she plays on the first appearance of that time of day. Same framing, scale, outline and background rules as every other sprite. She likes the small hours best, so the late ones should read as her natural habitat.

POSES (one image each, exact filename):
greet_morning.png — Morning (5–11): holding a small cup of tea in one hand, her folded fan in the other, a warm just-woke-up smile, head tilted, cap sitting slightly askew.
greet_noon.png — Noon (11–14): holding a small rice ball in one hand and her folded fan in the other, cheeks a little full, a cheerful contented smile.
greet_afternoon.png — Afternoon (14–18): fanning herself slowly with the open fan, a relaxed drowsy smile, one eye half closed, head tilted to one side.
greet_evening.png — Evening (18–23): holding a small glowing paper lotus lantern in one hand and her folded fan in the other, a warm relaxed smile, sleeves falling softly.
greet_night.png — Late night (23–2, her favourite hour): an open book in one hand and her open fan in the other, absorbed and happy ("just one more chapter"), eyes bright, braid falling forward.
greet_dawn.png — Very late (2–5): hugging her closed fan against her chest with both hands, eyes half closed, a small sleepy smile, head tilted, cap sliding a little.
```
