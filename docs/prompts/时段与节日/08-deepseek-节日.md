# DeepSeek · 节日登场（8 张）

附件：`ref-deepseek.webp`

按日期顺序，万圣节最急（10-31）。

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, horns, ears, wings and tail included), so no white part of her ever touches the white background directly.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.

CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek, the blue whale maid girl, anime-girl personification of DeepSeek.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat (the name is a fond joke she protests). Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge on top.
- A large white frilly maid headdress; dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head; a light-blue ribbon bow on her right side (viewer's right).
- Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush.
- Navy maid dress: white frilly blouse with a navy bow tie and a small blue diamond brooch, navy waist belt with white buttons, white frilly apron with a small navy whale emblem, navy skirt with gold vine embroidery and small gold bows; navy shoes with white socks.
- A big dark-navy whale tail curling out from behind her on the viewer's right.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; arms and fists move a lot.

STYLE NOTE (DeepSeek): make every pose big and expressive. happy = jumping with both arms up, tail flicking up; oops = swirly eyes, hands on cheeks, tail drooping; thinking = ahoge bent into a question-mark shape; pat = tail wagging hard; tired = flopped, holding an empty rice bowl; proud = hands on hips, "hmph!" face.

FESTIVAL GREETINGS: each image below is a one-time greeting she plays on the first appearance of a holiday. Same framing, scale, outline and background rules as every other sprite.
FESTIVAL EXCEPTION to the format rules: for these images ONLY, she may wear festive accessories or a festive outer layer (a hat, cape, scarf, jacket) and hold a festive prop. Her face, hair color and style, ears / horns / wings / tail, and her signature hair ornament must stay exactly as in the reference so she is instantly recognizable. Still: NO text, NO letters or Chinese characters (no 福, no greetings, no numbers or years), no flags, no logos; background stays pure white with no scenery.

POSES (one image each, exact filename):
fest_halloween.png — Halloween (Oct 31): Dressed as a cute ghost: a white sheet draped over her head and shoulders with her whale-fin ears poking out through holes and her face visible, holding a small pumpkin candy bucket, a big playful 'boo!' grin.
fest_christmas.png — Christmas (Dec 25): A Santa hat, carrying a small cloth sack of presents over her shoulder (a rice bowl peeking out of the sack), cheeks flushed, big cheerful grin.
fest_newyear.png — New Year's Day (Jan 1): A party hat, blowing a party horn (the paper horn unrolling), jumping with one arm up, tail up.
fest_spring.png — Spring Festival / Chinese New Year: A red festive jacket, holding a bowl of steaming dumplings with chopsticks, cheeks stuffed, very happy.
fest_lantern.png — Lantern Festival: Holding a big bowl of tangyuan, one on the spoon, eyes sparkling.
fest_dragonboat.png — Dragon Boat Festival: Hugging a big zongzi with both arms, drooling slightly, tail wagging.
fest_midautumn.png — Mid-Autumn Festival: Holding a mooncake with a big bite taken out of it, crumbs on her cheek, happy.
fest_national.png — National Day Golden Week (Oct 1): holiday travel theme, NOT political, no flags: Sunglasses, a big backpack with a rice cooker strapped to it, excited travel pose.
```
