```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, horns, ears, wings and tail included), so no white part of her ever touches the white background directly.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.

CHARACTER LOCK — Gemini, the starry cat girl, anime-girl personification of Gemini (Google).
Personality: bright, playful, mischievous, curious and quick; a little smug (the ":3" cat smile); the "twin" theme shows as two-sided moods, from teasing to sweet.
Keep exactly:
- Long voluminous wavy hair, blue-violet at the top fading to pink-magenta at the tips; one curled ahoge; two small side buns.
- Blue-violet cat ears with pink inner fur and white fluff.
- Heterochromia: HER RIGHT eye (viewer's left) is pink-magenta, HER LEFT eye (viewer's right) is amber-gold. Keep this exactly.
- A large iridescent four-pointed star hairclip with gold rim on her left side (viewer's right) with small dangling star charms; small star earrings.
- Navy crop-top with a high collar and star gems, white off-shoulder sleeves with navy cuffs, a violet sash, a navy skirt over a white frilly petticoat, iridescent four-pointed star charms hanging from the outfit, white leg warmers, navy-violet platform shoes.
- A fluffy blue-violet cat tail on the viewer's right.
How she shows emotion: cat body language. Ears perk up or flatten, tail puffs, curls or swishes; cat-like poses (paw hands, stretching like a cat). The ":3" smile is her default.

STYLE NOTE (Gemini): use cat body language. listening = ears perked straight up; thinking = ":3" mouth, tail curled into a question-mark shape; happy = paw-hands raised, tail up; oops = ears flattened, tail puffed; surprised = fur and tail fully puffed; pat = eyes closed, purring, leaning her head into the hand; stretch = cat-style stretch with paws forward; sleeping = curled tail, ears folded.

TIME-OF-DAY GREETINGS: each image below is a short greeting pose she plays when the viewer first opens their computer in that part of the day. Same framing, scale and outline rules as every other sprite. Props must be small and held close to the body so her face and silhouette stay clear. No sun, moon, stars, clocks or other scenery in the background: the background stays pure white.

POSES (one image each, exact filename):
greet_morning.png — Morning: rubbing one eye with a paw-hand, the other paw holding a small carton of milk with a straw, hair and tail slightly fluffed up from sleep, ears half-raised, a sleepy ':3'.
greet_noon.png — Noon: holding a fish-shaped taiyaki snack up with both paw-hands, about to take a bite, ears perked straight up, delighted ':3' face, tail curled up happily.
greet_afternoon.png — Afternoon play: batting at a small ball of violet yarn floating near one raised paw-hand (the loose yarn thread trails a little), playful smug look, tail swishing.
greet_evening.png — Evening: one arm raised pointing up and slightly to the side, as if pointing at the first star of the night, eyes sparkling, the iridescent star charms on her outfit catching the light, a bright excited smile, tail up.
greet_night.png — Late night: wide-awake night-cat mode, big round glowing eyes, crouched slightly forward with paw-hands raised playfully, a mischievous ':3', tail lashing; cats are most active at night.
greet_dawn.png — Very late (2-5am): wearing a small violet nightcap with a star tip, curled tail wrapped in front of her, kneading the air with both paw-hands like a cat on a soft blanket, half-closed content eyes, a sleepy ':3'.
```
