# Grok · 时段与节日（6 + 8 张）

附件：`docs/prompts/ref-grok.webp`。拼法：[00](00-定妆.md) 的「格式规则」→「角色锁定」→ 下面对应的那段。节日这批允许在原来的衣服上**加**节日小物件，王冠、蝙蝠蝴蝶结、金发双马尾不能丢；斧头可以放下不画（写了「no axe」的那几张）。

## 时段问候（6 张）

```
POSES (one image each, exact filename):
greet_morning.png   — Morning: holding a takeaway coffee cup (plain, no logo, no text) in one hand, the axe leaning on her shoulder, a sleepy but cocky fang grin, twin-tails a bit messy.
greet_noon.png      — Noon: biting a big sandwich held in both hands, cheeks full, the axe planted beside her, eyes looking at the viewer with a "what?" expression.
greet_afternoon.png — Afternoon: sipping a glass of red berry juice through a straw (plain glass, no logo) like a little vampire, leaning on the planted axe, a satisfied grin.
greet_evening.png   — Evening: holding the axe upright in one hand, its haft resting against her shoulder, waving with two fingers as if heading out for the night, a lively fang grin.
greet_night.png     — Night (her favorite time): perched with the axe hugged in one arm, a small black phone glowing on her face from below, a mischievous late-night grin, bat-wing bows spread wide.
greet_dawn.png      — Very late (2-5am): dark circles under her eyes, still scrolling a small phone held close to her face, leaning on the axe, a tired stubborn smirk, ahoge drooping.
```
