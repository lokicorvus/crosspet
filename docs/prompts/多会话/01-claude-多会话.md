# Claude · 手忙脚乱（4 张）

附件：`ref-claude.webp`

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, horns, ears, wings and tail included), so no white part of her ever touches the white background directly.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.

CHARACTER LOCK — Claude, the anime-girl personification of Claude (Anthropic).
Personality: gentle, calm, bookish, a little shy, quietly cheerful; she thinks before she speaks.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy copper-orange hair, loose side-swept bangs, warm amber eyes.
- An orange daisy-like hair flower with a black striped ribbon bow and a white tassel on HER LEFT side (viewer's right); small drop earrings.
- Cream-white dress with a short cape collar, orange flower embroidery, orange and black front panels, small black bows with gold tassels, a black waist sash, an orange flower brooch at the collar; dark brown boots.
- Signature prop: a dark brown hardcover book with an orange flower emblem. Keep it in every image unless the pose says otherwise.
How she shows emotion: through the book (hugging it, hiding behind it, reading it) and small, soft expressions. Never wild or loud.

MULTI-TASKING ANIMATION FRAMES: she is handling several jobs at once. The app flips quickly between the LEFT frame and the RIGHT frame, so she looks like she is turning back and forth, busy on one side, then the other. Each image must read as "mid-action, a bit hectic", never as a static pose.

POSE EXCEPTION to the format rules (for these images only): she does NOT have to stand straight and face the viewer. She may lean, twist her body toward one side, turn her head, lift one foot slightly off balance, and her hair, ribbons, wings and tail may swing with the motion. Keep only these layout rules: the whole character is inside the image, same scale as the reference (about 90% of the image height), feet near the bottom, roughly centered.

KEEP IT CUTE AND GRACEFUL (very important):
- NO T-pose, NO arms spread straight out wide, NO raised-arms "surrender" pose, NO armpits showing. Elbows stay naturally bent; hands work close to her body, at most reaching a little past her shoulders.
- Asymmetric and natural: one hand actively working, the other reaching or steadying something; head turned toward the side she is working on.
- Expressions stay small and pretty: a flustered pout, pressed lips, a small "wah" mouth, a sweat drop on the cheek. NO wide-open screaming mouth, no ugly grimace.
- Every prop must TOUCH her or her hands (things can be tipping or slipping, but still touching). Nothing floats detached in the air: the app deletes detached pieces. A few short motion lines drawn right against her arm or hair are fine.

TWO-FRAME CONSISTENCY:
- Draw the "-left" image first. Then draw the "-right" image as an EDIT of the "-left" image you just made: same character, same outfit, same props, same scale and position. Only the direction changes: in "-right" she turns toward the viewer's RIGHT and the working hand / steadying hand swap roles.
- Do NOT simply mirror-flip the image: her hair ornament and other one-sided details must stay on the same side as in the reference.
- "left" and "right" mean the viewer's left and right.

POSES (one image each, exact filename, draw them in this order):
busy-left.png — Two jobs at once: body turned toward the viewer's LEFT, reading an open book held up on that side with one hand, while her other hand reaches back to steady a second open book that is starting to tip on the right side (still touching her fingers). Pressed lips, eyes darting, one small sweat drop, hair swinging slightly.
busy-right.png — Same as busy-left but turned toward the viewer's RIGHT: now reading the book on the right, the other hand steadying the tipping book on the left.
swamped-left.png — Too many jobs: hugging a small stack of three books against her chest with one arm, the top book sliding off toward the viewer's LEFT and caught by her fingertips, body leaning that way, one foot lifted, small swirl eyes, a wobbly little 'awawa' mouth, blushing, a few strands of hair loose.
swamped-right.png — Same as swamped-left but the top book is sliding toward the viewer's RIGHT and she leans and catches it on that side.
```
