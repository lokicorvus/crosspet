# 角色生图提示词

给 Codex（或其他能看参考图的生图 AI）用，画四个内置角色的各个姿态：Claude · GPT · DeepSeek · Gemini。
画好后按 [自定义角色](../自定义角色.md) 里的步骤导入。

## 怎么用

1. **一次只画一个角色**。每次发给 Codex 的内容按这个顺序拼：
   - 「通用格式规则」
   - 该角色的「角色锁定」
   - 「姿态清单」里你这次要画的那几行
   
   附件只附**这个角色自己的参考图**，不要混发：

   | 角色 | 参考图 |
   |---|---|
   | Claude | `ref-claude.webp` |
   | GPT | `ref-gpt.webp` |
   | DeepSeek | `ref-deepseek.webp` |
   | Gemini | `ref-gemini.webp` |

2. **每批先画 1 张试稿**，确认没画走样，再画剩下的。
3. **`idle-blink` 一定要说明是「编辑参考图」**，只改眼睛。就算它整张重画了也没关系，`tools/fix_blink.py` 会只把眼睛那块贴回原图。
4. 画好的图放到 `characters/<角色>/raw/`（文件名就用下面清单里的名字）：

   | 角色 | 文件夹名 |
   |---|---|
   | Claude | `claude` |
   | GPT | `gpt` |
   | DeepSeek | `deepseek` |
   | Gemini | `gemini` |

   文件名就用清单里给的名字。

**A 档（先画，最常出现）**：idle-blink、idle-2、listening、thinking、reading、writing、running、happy、oops、pat、sleeping

**B 档**：searching、delegating、proud、surprised、tired

**C 档**：hum、stretch、yawn、idle-3、drawing（画画，识别到画图工具时用）

## 通用格式规则（每次都贴在最前面）

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF). Nothing else: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, horns, ears, wings and tail included), so no white part of her ever touches the white background directly.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.
```

## 角色锁定（每个角色一段，按需贴）

### Claude · Claude

```
CHARACTER LOCK — Claude, the anime-girl personification of Claude (Anthropic).
Personality: gentle, calm, bookish, a little shy, quietly cheerful; she thinks before she speaks.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy copper-orange hair, loose side-swept bangs, warm amber eyes.
- An orange daisy-like hair flower with a black striped ribbon bow and a white tassel on HER LEFT side (viewer's right); small drop earrings.
- Cream-white dress with a short cape collar, orange flower embroidery, orange and black front panels, small black bows with gold tassels, a black waist sash, an orange flower brooch at the collar; dark brown boots.
- Signature prop: a dark brown hardcover book with an orange flower emblem. Keep it in every image unless the pose says otherwise.
How she shows emotion: through the book (hugging it, hiding behind it, reading it) and small, soft expressions. Never wild or loud.
```

### GPT · GPT（Codex）

```
CHARACTER LOCK — GPT, the white dragon girl, anime-girl personification of GPT / ChatGPT (OpenAI).
Personality: composed, elegant, mature "onee-san" (cool big-sister) aura; reliable and precise; rarely flustered, and when she is, she tries to hide it. Dry, understated humor.
Keep exactly:
- Very long, wavy, white hair with a soft lavender tint in the shadows; one curled ahoge; pointed elf-like ears.
- Two large white ridged dragon horns curving up from her head.
- Pale lavender-grey eyes with a calm, half-lidded look.
- A white interlaced-knot hair ornament on her left side (viewer's right) with a long white tassel earring.
- Small white bat-like dragon wings on her back; a long, thick white dragon tail covered in scales, curling out on the viewer's right.
- White sleeveless qipao-style dress with crystal diamond ornaments down the front, white interlaced-knot ornaments at the waist with tassels, long translucent white flowing sleeves and an open translucent white over-skirt; white shoes with knot ornaments.
Palette: almost entirely white and pale lavender; keep it soft, NEVER add strong colors.
How she shows emotion: through wings and tail (wings spread when happy or proud, droop when tired, tail curls or flicks) and very small facial changes. Even "happy" is a confident small smile, not a wide grin.
```

### DeepSeek · DeepSeek

```
CHARACTER LOCK — DeepSeek, the blue whale maid girl, anime-girl personification of DeepSeek.
Personality: cheerful, energetic, curious; secretly a bit lazy; tsundere and sweet-talking; loves white rice; absolutely refuses to be called fat (the name is a fond joke she protests). Big, open, readable expressions.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy hair, deep indigo/navy blue fading to bright blue at the tips; one curled ahoge on top.
- A large white frilly maid headdress; dark navy whale-fin ears on both sides of her head; a light-blue ribbon bow on her right side (viewer's right).
- Big round bright-blue eyes, pink blush.
- Navy maid dress: white frilly blouse with a navy bow tie and a small blue diamond brooch, navy waist belt with white buttons, white frilly apron with a small navy whale emblem, navy skirt with gold vine embroidery and small gold bows; navy shoes with white socks.
- A big dark-navy whale tail curling out from behind her on the viewer's right.
How she shows emotion: with her whole body. Tail wags, droops or flicks; the ahoge bends into shapes; arms and fists move a lot.
```

### Gemini · Gemini

```
CHARACTER LOCK — Gemini, the starry cat girl, anime-girl personification of Gemini (Google).
Personality: bright, playful, mischievous, curious and quick; a little smug (the ":3" cat smile); the "twin" theme shows as two-sided moods, from teasing to sweet.
Keep exactly:
- Long voluminous wavy hair, blue-violet at the top fading to pink-magenta at the tips; one curled ahoge; two small side buns.
- Blue-violet cat ears with pink inner fur and white fluff.
- Heterochromia: HER RIGHT eye (viewer's left) is pink-magenta, HER LEFT eye (viewer's right) is amber-gold. Keep this exactly.
- A large iridescent four-pointed star hairclip with gold rim on her left side (viewer's right) with small dangling star charms; small star earrings.
- Navy crop-top with a high collar and star gems, white off-shoulder sleeves with navy cuffs, a violet sash, a navy skirt over a white frilly petticoat, iridescent four-pointed star charms hanging from the outfit, white leg warmers, navy-violet platform shoes.
- A fluffy blue-violet cat tail on the viewer's right.
How she shows emotion: cat body language. Ears perk up or flatten, tail puffs, curls or swishes; cat-like poses (paw hands, stretching like a cat). The ":3" smile is her default.
```

## 姿态清单（四个角色通用）

把要画的那几行贴在「角色锁定」后面。`<prop>` 指角色的标志道具：Claude是书；其他三位没有道具，用手和尾巴、翅膀、耳朵来表现。

```
POSES (draw only the ones listed below, one image each, exact filename):

idle-blink.png — an EDIT of the attached reference: change ONLY the eyes to softly closed. Everything else must stay pixel-identical (it is swapped in for a blink animation).
idle-2.png     — same standing framing, looking slightly to one side, faint relaxed smile; hair, tail or wings drifting slightly.
idle-3.png     — same framing, head tilted, hands together in front, calm content expression.
listening.png  — attentive: leaning slightly forward, eyes a little wider and sparkling, small open mouth ("hm?"), one hand raised a little.
thinking.png   — one finger on chin or cheek, eyes looking up and to the side, small pondering mouth.
reading.png    — reading intently: Claude reads her open book; the others read a small floating open book or a scroll, held in both hands.
writing.png    — writing with a white feather quill in a small notebook or on the <prop>; focused, tiny smile.
running.png    — determined and busy: sleeves pushed up, one small fist raised ("let's do this!"), slightly leaning into motion.
searching.png  — holding a round brass magnifying glass up to one eye, curious.
delegating.png — smiling and waving goodbye with one hand, as if sending a helper off.
happy.png      — very happy: eyes closed in a smile, mouth open, light blush, arms up or hugging the <prop>.
proud.png      — proud and pleased: chin up, eyes closed, small confident smile, one hand on hip.
oops.png       — flustered: worried wide eyes, sweat drop on the face, shoulders raised, hands near the cheeks.
surprised.png  — startled: eyes wide open, mouth a small "o", leaning back a little.
pat.png        — being patted on the head: eyes closed, blissful, blushing, shoulders up, slightly ducking.
sleeping.png   — dozing while standing: eyes closed, head drooped to one side, peaceful.
tired.png      — exhausted: droopy half-closed eyes, slouched shoulders, arms hanging low.
hum.png        — eyes closed with a gentle smile, swaying, humming.
stretch.png    — stretching with both arms raised above the head, eyes closed.
yawn.png       — yawning with one hand covering the mouth, eyes closed, a tiny tear at the eye corner.
drawing.png    — painting: holding a paintbrush in one hand and a small wooden palette (with a few paint dabs) in the other, focused, gentle smile, as if painting a picture in front of her.
```

### 各角色的风格补充

把对应那一行加在姿态清单后面，让同一个动作带上角色自己的味道：

```
STYLE NOTE (Claude): keep every pose soft and modest; the book appears in almost every pose (hugged, held, read, hidden behind).
```
```
STYLE NOTE (GPT): keep her elegant and restrained. happy = confident closed-eye smile with wings slightly spread; proud = arms crossed, wings fully spread, tail raised; oops = a single sweat drop and a slightly embarrassed sideways glance, never panicking; pat = she pretends to be annoyed, blushing, eyes looking away, tail wagging anyway; tired = wings drooping, tail limp; sleeping = curled tail wrapped in front of her feet.
```
```
STYLE NOTE (DeepSeek): make every pose big and expressive. happy = jumping with both arms up, tail flicking up; oops = swirly eyes, hands on cheeks, tail drooping; thinking = ahoge bent into a question-mark shape; pat = tail wagging hard; tired = flopped, holding an empty rice bowl; proud = hands on hips, "hmph!" face.
```
```
STYLE NOTE (Gemini): use cat body language. listening = ears perked straight up; thinking = ":3" mouth, tail curled into a question-mark shape; happy = paw-hands raised, tail up; oops = ears flattened, tail puffed; surprised = fur and tail fully puffed; pat = eyes closed, purring, leaning her head into the hand; stretch = cat-style stretch with paws forward; sleeping = curled tail, ears folded.
```

## DeepSeek 专属动作（可选）

DeepSeek 的配置里已经预留了这些专属动作，**画好对应立绘就会自动启用**，没画的不影响。发给 Codex 时附 `ref-deepseek.webp`，前面照常贴「通用格式规则」和 DeepSeek 的「角色锁定」。

```
EXTRA POSES for DeepSeek (one image each, exact filename):

eat.png        — happily eating: holding a small bowl of steaming white rice in one hand and chopsticks in the other, cheeks puffed, eyes closed in bliss, tail wagging. (She loves white rice.)
slack.png      — slacking off: sitting or slumped lazily with her chin resting on her folded arms, half-closed sleepy eyes, a tiny content smile, tail lying flat. (She is secretly lazy.)
swim.png       — swimming pose: one arm stretched forward and one back as if doing a breaststroke in mid-air, cheerful face, hair and tail flowing behind her.
deepthink.png  — deep thinking: eyes closed, both index fingers pressed to her temples, a focused serious face, ahoge curled tightly, tail curled around her feet.
pout.png       — tsundere pout after being called fat: puffed cheeks, arms crossed, looking away with a blush, an angry little frown, tail flicking. ("I'm NOT fat!")
```

| 动作 | 什么时候出现 |
|---|---|
| `eat` / `slack` / `swim` | 闲着时偶尔出现（比内置的哼歌、伸懒腰更常见） |
| `deepthink` | 思考超过 20 秒，身边冒深海泡泡 |
| `pout` | 被戳时（「不许叫我胖！」） |

## 大成功：戴皇冠的 proud（四个角色各一张）

「大活干完」时用的 `proud`，改成皇冠直接画在她头上、按角色特点设计。画好后**覆盖**原来的 `proud.png`，App 会自动不再叠画特效皇冠。

照常先贴「通用格式规则」和该角色的「角色锁定」，再贴下面对应的一段。皇冠是戴在头上的道具，不算「漂浮的符号」，但要整顶都在画面里。

```
EXTRA POSE (exact filename): proud.png
She has just finished a big job and is wearing a small crown, very pleased with herself. The crown sits ON her head (not floating), fully inside the image with a little margin above it; scale her down slightly if needed so crown and feet both fit.
```

接着贴她自己的那一行：

```
Claude:   A delicate gold tiara woven from small orange daisies (matching her hair flower) with a tiny amber gem in the middle. She holds her book up against her chest with both arms, eyes closed in a soft, proud smile, a light blush. Modest and graceful, not loud.
GPT:      A white-gold crown shaped like a row of small dragon horns, set with clear crystal diamonds matching the ones on her dress, sitting between her real horns. Arms crossed, chin slightly raised, a confident half-lidded smirk; wings spread wide, tail curled up proudly.
DeepSeek: A slightly-too-big gold crown with sapphire wave patterns and a tiny whale-spout ornament on top, tilted to one side. Fists on hips, chest puffed out, a huge toothy grin with eyes closed, ahoge standing straight up, tail wagging. Optionally a pair of chopsticks held up like a scepter.
Gemini:   A crown made of iridescent four-pointed stars (matching her star hairclip) with her cat ears poking through gaps in it. One paw-hand next to her face, winking, the ":3" smile, tail curled into a happy hook.
```

## 社区梗动作

### GPT：reset（额度提前重置）

Codex 社区的老梗：官方时不时给大家「reset」额度，或者用了重置卡，额度在该重置之前就满了。CrossPet 发现 Codex 额度在预定的重置时间之前突然大幅恢复，就会播这个动作。

```
EXTRA POSE for GPT (exact filename): reset.png
She has just used a "reset card": holding up a glowing pale-blue playing-card-sized card in one hand at shoulder height (the card shows only a simple circular-arrow emblem, no letters), her usual cool face broken into a rare, wide-eyed delighted smile with sparkling eyes, wings flared up in surprise, tail swishing.
```

### DeepSeek：feast（余额增加，大口吃白饭）

DeepSeek 的余额变多（充值了）时播放。

```
EXTRA POSE for DeepSeek (exact filename): feast.png
Celebrating with a feast: holding a HUGE bowl heaped with a mountain of white rice, bigger than her head, shoveling rice into her mouth with chopsticks, cheeks stuffed round like a hamster, eyes squeezed shut in pure bliss, a few grains of rice on her cheek, tail wagging hard, ahoge bouncing.
```

| 动作 | 什么时候出现 |
|---|---|
| GPT `reset` | Codex 某个额度窗口在预定重置时间之前突然恢复了一大截（需要打开「显示 GPT 额度」） |
| DeepSeek `feast` | DeepSeek 余额比上次多了（充值） |

没画立绘时，这两个动作会先用 `happy` / `eat` 顶上。
